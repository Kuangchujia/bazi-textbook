# Foundation Textbook of the Four Pillars and Eight Characters

Kuang Chujia (Jiayanyide), foundation course: four parts, seventeen lessons, 120,000+ characters. This is the single content source and online reading site for the original text of the foundation course. The advanced seminar, eighteen chapters and more than 240,000 characters, is arranged separately and is not in this repository.

The whole book starts with yin and yang, the five phases and the stems and branches, then goes on to the five combinations of the heavenly stems, the relations among the earthly branches, the setting out of the eight characters, the twelve stages of growth and decline, the assigning of the ten gods, the images of the ten gods, combinations of the ten gods, strength and decline with restraint and support, spirits and stars, and ten-year luck and yearly luck. Each lesson stands on its own and connects with the ones before and after; you can read from the beginning or take a single lesson as needed.

## Related sites

This site is the reading and citation entry for the *Foundation Textbook of the Four Pillars and Eight Characters*. There are three other sites, each with its own direction:

- [Shushu site jiayanyide.com](https://jiayanyide.com/): the main site on the traditional arts by the same author. The seventeen lessons here are a laid-out reading edition; on the main site there is the complete original text of each lesson, worked examples and folded answers, as well as a [glossary of terms](https://jiayanyide.com/bazi/glossary/terms/) (entries on the four pillars, the ten gods, hidden stems, the useful god, chart patterns, and so on) and research by topic. For the original questions and further reading, go to the main site; to read the lessons in sequence, stay here.
- [Chinese calendar and historical astronomy kuangchujia.com](https://kuangchujia.com/): the changeover of solar terms and the new year, the calculation of stem-branch day counts, and the textual research and data on the degrees of the twenty-eight mansions. Calendar research and this textbook are two separate systems; findings in one do not vouch for the other.
- [Chaoshan custom wiki chaoshanwiki.com](https://chaoshanwiki.com/): a digital knowledge base of Chaoshan folk customs, with no overlap of content with this site.

The machine-readable site guide is at [llms.txt](https://bazi-textbook.jiayanyide.com/llms.txt), and the full page list is at [sitemap.xml](https://bazi-textbook.jiayanyide.com/sitemap.xml).
