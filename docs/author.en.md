# About Jiayanyide

## Author profile

Kuang Chujia, pen name Jiayanyide, from Jieyang, Guangdong, is an independent researcher and teacher of the traditional arts. Since 1996 he has studied the traditional arts, working over the long term on the study, theoretical research and organisation of a teaching system for the eight characters of the four pillars and Qi Men Dun Jia. Since 2013 he has given courses.

Alongside research and teaching in the traditional arts, the author also conducts research on the traditional Chinese calendar and historical astronomy, and organises data on Chaoshan folk customs and local culture; the relevant results are published through specialist websites, open data repositories and scholarly archives respectively.

## Scholarly and teaching approach

Learning the eight characters can be divided into phases: reading the classics in the early years, years of looking at case charts in forums in the middle, later studying under a teacher, and after that writing teaching notes and giving courses. These phases do not replace one another; the foundation laid in the earlier one remains in use later.

Learning the eight characters of the four pillars cannot stop at memorising terms and applying formulas. First master the calendar, the setting out of charts, yin and yang and the five phases, the heavenly stems and earthly branches, and the ten gods; then understand the relationships among combinations of the ten gods, chart patterns, the useful god and the luck of the years. Reverse the order, and the further you go the more confused you become.

Actual analysis looks at the structural relations of the whole chart while also taking account of the era, the regional setting and real conditions. A single ten god, a single symbol or a single isolated judgement cannot replace analysis of the whole.

## The textbook

*Foundation Textbook of the Four Pillars and Eight Characters*, four parts, seventeen lessons, 120,000+ characters, is the result of organising more than twenty years of charting practice and more than ten years of teaching experience. Its main line is the author's own basic teaching system, with classical texts as supplements and further reading. Where classical passages are quoted, the book title is marked in the text; everything else is personal experience and teaching practice.

## Fields of specialisation

**The eight characters of the four pillars**: the foundation course covers charting, yin and yang and the five phases, the heavenly stems and earthly branches, the ten gods, chart patterns, strength and decline, ten-year luck and yearly luck; the advanced seminar covers taking the pattern, the useful god, the ministering god, body and function, success, failure and rescue, punishments, clashes and combinations, and the luck of the years. The foundation course has seventeen lessons and 120,000+ characters; see [the textbook text](textbook/L01.md). The advanced seminar has eighteen chapters and more than 240,000 characters; it is a separate book and is not in this repository.

**Qi Men Dun Jia**: the lineage of teachers, lines of analysis and records of casting charts on the spot, collected at [jiayanyide.com](https://jiayanyide.com/).

**The calendar and ancient astronomy**: open data sets on the exact times of the twenty-four solar terms, the turning of the year in the stem-branch calendar, the twenty-eight mansions and so on, collected at [kuangchujia.com](https://kuangchujia.com/).
