# Lesson 14 · Strength and Support: the Basic Concepts

[简体中文](/textbook/L14/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L14/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L14/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L14/)

## 1. The schools of the eight characters at a glance

Before coming to strength properly, it is worth knowing the four schools of eight-character reading. This course takes strength and support as its core; seasonal adjustment, pattern and disease-and-remedy are given as background, touched on but not pursued.

| School | Chief names | Chief texts | Core idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strength and support | Ren Tieqiao, Li Hanchen | *Ditiansui Chanwei* | A strong day stem is checked, a weak one supported; where the force is extreme, follow it |
| Seasonal adjustment | — | *Qiongtong Baojian* | Reading by the nature of the stem and the cold, warmth, dryness and damp of the season: fire in winter cold, water in summer heat |
| Pattern | Shen Xiaozhan | *Zi Ping Zhen Quan* | The month sets the pattern, and a supporting useful god is taken as its minister |
| Disease and remedy | Zhang Shenfeng | *Shenfeng Tongkao* | A pattern with a disease and a remedy is the highest; it settles the question of using the four fortunate and four unlucky with or against the flow |

Each of the four has its strength and its limit. At the beginning the value is in going deep into one, and a mixture is the worst thing for it: the thread of thought tangles and the study suffers. The direction this course will take later is the pattern method; what is taught now — strength and support, the generating and checking of stems and branches — is the common ground all four schools stand on. A good foundation first, then the tall building.

> A word on the following patterns. In the strength-and-support school, where the force is extreme, follow it, means that when the day stem is at its strongest or its weakest one does not apply the ordinary rule of check and support but goes with the extreme: this is advanced material, and lesson 15 sets out the two special patterns, the strongest of the strong and the weakest of the weak.

## 2. The own party and the other party

> From lesson 8: the own party is the seal and the peers, what generates me and what helps me; the other party is eating and wounding, wealth, and officer and killing, what I generate, what I check and what checks me.

### 2.1 The two parties

| Party | Gods in it | Effect on the day stem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The own party, which makes me strong | what generates me, the seal; what helps me, the peers | many of the own party and the day stem grows strong |
| The other party, which makes me weak | what I generate, eating and wounding; what I check, wealth; what checks me, officer and killing | many of the other party and the day stem grows weak |

### 2.2 The rule

- Much that generates me, or that helps me, and with force: the day stem is strong.
- Much that checks me, much that I generate, much that I check: the day stem is weak.
- Little that generates or helps me, or none of it, or without force: the day stem is weak.

> One body many faces: the division into own party and other party is the first step in weighing force. The same support, peers and seal, flowers on brocade when the self is strong and even too much of it, charcoal in the snow when the self is weak and help that works: one substance, different force, different conclusions.

## 3. Strength by season, strength by weight

> From the *Zi Ping Zhen Quan*, on strength: in season is strong, out of season is weak; many of one’s party is weight, few to help is lightness. And so there are those strong yet light, and those weak yet heavy. — Judging strength is the first gate of the art; past that gate the door of the reading is properly open.

### 3.1 Judging the season

Strength here means the force of the day stem itself. The core reference is the state of the day stem among the twelve stages in the branches, and of those the month is decisive: the month is the outline of the chart and sets the temper of the whole.

- Strong: in the month branch the day stem is at birth, at lin-guan or at its peak.
- Weak: none of those states in the month branch.

> A caution: birth does not count as strong for a yin stem. Yi wood at the wu month is at birth, but it is not counted strong; a yin stem is strong only at lin-guan and at its peak. See lesson 15.

The lin-guan and peak branches of each stem, for reference:

| Stem | lin-guan (lu) | peak (blade) | birth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| jia wood | yin | mao | hai |
| yi wood | mao | yin | wu |
| bing fire | si | wu | yin |
| ding fire | wu | si | you |
| wu earth | si | wu | yin |
| ji earth | wu | si | you |
| geng metal | shen | you | si |
| xin metal | you | shen | zi |
| ren water | hai | zi | shen |
| gui water | zi | hai | mao |

> A yang stem is strong at birth, at lin-guan and at its peak. A yin stem is not strong at birth, but only at lin-guan and at its peak.

### 3.2 Judging the weight

Weight is relative: it compares the day stem, the seal included, with the other gods. Season looks at the state in the month; weight looks at the balance of force across the whole chart. The table sets out the difference.

| Concept | Definition | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A strong self | the force of the own party against the other party is the greater | the day stem at birth, lin-guan or peak in the branches, and much seal |
| A weak self | eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing are stronger than the day stem | the day stem in a strong state, but eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing stronger still |

Put simply: a strong self has ground under it, and seal and peers come to help; a weak self is outweighed by what drains it, eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing.

### 3.3 How the two stand to each other

- A strong self means a strong day stem: where the self is strong the day stem is certainly in force.
- Strong is not weight: a strong season does not mean the self outweighs the rest, and eating, wounding, wealth, officer and killing may be stronger still — see the case in lesson 15, ren born in the zi month, a yang blade pattern, and yet a weak self.
- A weak self is not always out of season: the day stem may still be in a strong state, with the month giving it force and the other party overwhelming it.
- Out of season is not lightness: season is the state in the month, weight is the balance across the chart, and the two are judged apart.

> A necessary warning: a strong chart is not thereby rich or honoured; a weak chart is not thereby poor or mean; and a chart neither strong nor weak, the balanced chart, is not thereby a good fate.
> A note by Chujia Kuang. In learning to read, what matters is keeping season, strength and weight apart. Many who never learn the art fail at exactly this: the concepts are confused. In study, in practice and in reading, the concepts and the reasoning must be clean and never muddled, and only so can accuracy be raised and held.

### 3.4 The seal is not the day stem

> Chujia Kuang, from practice: the seal is force applied from outside, and a strong seal is not a strong self. With the day stem rooted, the seal coming to generate it is flowers on brocade; with the day stem unrooted, no amount of seal will serve, for there is no root to feed. The commonest mistake in practice is to see a chart full of seal and call the self strong.

- A strong seal is not a strong self: the seal in force but the day stem unrooted, with no birth, lin-guan or peak, is still a weak self.
- Too much wood and the fire goes out: much seal and the day stem resting, and the self is weak.
- The classical statements: metal leans on earth to be born, and much earth buries the metal; water leans on metal to be born, and much metal muddies the water; wood leans on water to be born, and much water floats the wood.

> From the *Jishan Pian*: wood is nourished by water, and where the water is abundant the wood drifts; metal leans on earth to be born, and where the earth is thick the metal is buried. — A seal over-strong with the day stem unrooted does not generate the self but presses it down, and this is the classical statement that a strong seal is not a strong self. The order of judgment: the root first, the seal after. The root is inner force, the seal outer; where the inner is insufficient, no amount of outer force will hold it up.

## 4. The four kinds of useful god

Taking the useful god is the practical step that follows the analysis of strength: the point of judging strength is to choose the right useful god. The four kinds below each have their place, and at the foundation stage the useful god of support and check is the main one.

### 4.1 The useful god of support and check

The commonest method of all: check the strong, support the weak.

| Case | Useful god | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A strong self | officer and killing, which check; eating and wounding, which drain; wealth, which consumes | check the strong and hold back the swelling |
| A weak self | the seal, which generates; the peers, which help | support the weak and lend a hand |

### 4.2 The useful god of seasonal adjustment

Support and check settles the balance of force, but there is another case: the five phases neither leaning nor lacking, the self neither strong nor weak, and still nothing comes of the chart. The trouble then is temperature and moisture. Born in winter it is too cold and all things are frozen; born in summer it is too dry and all things wither.

The principle: the five phases may be balanced and the climate still intemperate, and there is no life in it. One god of adjustment, and the chart is brought to a middle warmth.

| Season | The trouble | The adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Born in winter, metal cold and water chill | water in force and the qi cold, all things dormant | fire as the god of adjustment, to warm the chart and thaw it |
| Born in summer, fire in force and earth dry | fire in force and the qi dry, all things shrivelled | water as the god of adjustment, to moisten the dry earth |

Seasonal adjustment ranks high: where it conflicts with the god of support and check, adjustment may still come first. But at the foundation stage strength and support are the main thing, and adjustment is enough to know as a supplement.

### 4.3 The useful god that bridges

The bridging god settles a stand-off: two phases in the chart evenly matched, each checking the other, neither giving way. Then neither side is helped; a middleman is found instead, turning the check into a generation and the enemies into friends.

The principle: two phases that will not yield, and the phase between them that can carry the flow is taken to bridge.

| The two sides | The bridging god | The effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wood and earth in equal part | fire | wood generates fire, fire generates earth: the check becomes a generation |
| metal and fire even | earth | fire generates earth, earth generates metal: enemies become friends |
| water and fire at odds | wood | water generates wood, wood generates fire: the clash becomes a harmony |

### 4.4 The useful god of disease and remedy

The principle: find the disease in the chart and write the prescription. Where the useful god is checked and hurt, what hurts it is the disease. A chart with a disease and a remedy is a chart of the first order; with a disease and no remedy, distinction is hard to come by in a lifetime.

> From the *Wuyan Dubu*: it is the chart with a disease that is precious; without a hurt there is nothing uncommon; where the disease is taken out of the pattern, wealth and rank follow gladly.

The disease-and-remedy school looks elsewhere than the strength-and-support school: one watches the balance of the day stem, the other the seat of the trouble in the pattern, as when the four fortunate are checked or the four unlucky uncontrolled. At the foundation stage support and check come first, the pattern method is the direction later, and disease-and-remedy thinking has its application in the advanced class.

> Chujia Kuang, from practice: sorting the useful gods into kinds is for the convenience of learning them. In use the theories run into one another. Wealth robbed, and using the officer to check the rob and guard the wealth is a god of disease and remedy; using the eating god to drain the rob and generate the wealth is a god that bridges. One chart, another angle, another solution. So this section is enough to know, and there is no need to insist on which kind a god is.

## 5. Season, ground and company

> From the *Zi Ping Zhen Quan*, on the month: for the useful god of a chart, look to the month alone. — The month is the outline of the chart, and whether it is in season is the first ground of judgment.

Season is the gift of heaven, the state in the month; ground is the gift of earth, being rooted; company is the gift of men, the help that comes. The three are judged together, and no one of them settles it alone.

### 5.1 In season and out

The month is the season: the climate at birth, which sets the day stem’s strength before anything else.

| State | Definition | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In season | the day stem in a strong state in the month | the month branch at the day stem’s lin-guan or peak, or at birth for a yang stem |
| Out of season | the day stem taking no force from the month | the month branch where the day stem is resting, imprisoned or dead |

Different stems are in season in different months: the prosperous month is the proper case, and the strong month is one that generates the day stem — not in season directly, but fed by the month, and next in force.

| Day stem | Prosperous month, in season | Strong month, fed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia and yi wood | yin and mao | hai and zi |
| bing and ding fire | si and wu | yin and mao |
| wu and ji earth | si and wu | chen, xu, chou, wei |
| geng and xin metal | shen and you | chen, xu, chou, wei |
| ren and gui water | hai and zi | shen and you |

> A caution: wu earth born in the chen or xu month is strong, and ji earth in the chou or wei month. But wu born in chou or wei does not root in the chief qi of the month, and is not counted strong; ji earth the same.

???+ "Appendix: the five phases through the four seasons"
    The table of the five phases season by season, to make plain what resting, imprisoned and dead mean.
    | Season | prosperous | strong | resting | imprisoned | dead |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Spring (yin and mao months) | wood | fire | water | metal | earth |
    | Summer (si and wu months) | fire | earth | wood | water | metal |
    | Autumn (shen and you months) | metal | water | earth | fire | wood |
    | Winter (hai and zi months) | water | wood | metal | earth | fire |
    | The last month of each season (chen, xu, chou, wei) | earth | metal | fire | wood | water |
    **Prosperous**: the qi in season, the strongest of all, which is to be in season. **Strong**: generated by the prosperous qi, next in force, which is to be fed. **Resting**: what generated the prosperous qi, its work done, withdrawn. **Imprisoned**: what checks the prosperous qi, shut up and powerless. **Dead**: what the prosperous qi checks, the weakest of all.

### 5.2 Rooted and unrooted

The branches are the ground: whether the stems have somewhere to root settles whether the force is solid.

- Rooted: the stem roots in the branch, in the chief qi, the middle qi, the remainder qi or a store, and in the year, month, day or hour branch alike.
- Unrooted: shown without substance, no root and no branch to hold it.

> Rooting and showing, from lesson 10: a stem with a hidden stem of its own kind in a branch is rooted; a hidden stem in a branch showing in the stems is showing. The chief qi gives the strongest root, the middle qi next, the remainder qi the least. A root in the month or the day branch weighs more than one in the year or the hour.

The branches in which each stem is rooted, the core tool for judging ground:

| Stem | Branches where it is rooted |
| --- | --- |
| jia wood | yin, mao, hai, wei |
| yi wood | yin, mao, chen, wei |
| bing fire | yin, si, wu, xu |
| ding fire | si, wu, wei, xu |
| wu earth | yin, chen, si, wu, xu |
| ji earth | chou, si, wu, wei |
| geng metal | chou, si, shen, you |
| xin metal | chou, shen, you, xu |
| ren water | chen, shen, hai, zi |
| gui water | chou, chen, hai, zi |

Roots differ in force: lin-guan and the peak are the strongest, birth next, a store the weakest — though a store may be the ground of a turnaround at the decisive moment.

### 5.3 With company and without

The stems are the company: how much help comes from the peers and the seal settles how much support there is.

| State | Condition | The test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| With company | peers showing in the stems and rooted in the branches, or the seal generating with force | helpers in number and with substance |
| Without company | few peers or seal in the stems, or shown without a root | a lone force, short of help |

> A note by Chujia Kuang. Season is what one is born with, given by the month; ground is one’s own root, whether there is one; company is help from outside, whether there are helpers. The three are judged together and not one may be dropped. The worst mistake is to look at the month alone: in season is not necessarily strong, and out of season is not necessarily weak.

### 5.4 A worked example

One chart, to show season, ground and company used together:

> The chart: jia wood as the day stem, born in the yin month, mao in the day branch, jia showing in the hour stem, geng in the year stem.

| Test | Finding | Ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season | in season | jia born in the yin month: yin is jia’s lin-guan, the month in force |
| Ground | rooted | mao in the day branch is jia’s peak, a root in the chief qi with force |
| Company | with company | jia showing in the hour stem, a peer lending help |
| Altogether | a strong self | season, ground and company all present: three of three, the day stem strong |

> If the geng metal in the year stem were rooted at its peak in a shen year branch, and xin showed in the month stem, then the other party, metal checking wood, might outweigh the jia wood, and even in season the chart would need weighing with care. That is where lesson 15 goes.

## 6. Useful, adverse, favourable, hostile and idle gods

> This section is for background and is not required for the course.

| Concept | Definition | In use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful god | the stem or branch, and so the god, that helps the day stem | judged through support and check, adjustment, bridging, disease and remedy together |
| Adverse god | the character that works against the day stem | the counterpart of the useful god |
| Favourable god | the phase the chart welcomes, the one that helps the useful god | it supports the useful god in doing its work |
| Hostile god | the stem or branch that generates or helps the adverse god | it works against the favourable god |
| Idle god | a god with little effect on the fate | met in the luck, it means a quiet passage |

> In use: practice works mainly with the useful, the adverse and the favourable god; the hostile and the idle are seldom called on.

## Summary of the lesson

1. The four schools — strength and support, checking the strong and supporting the weak; seasonal adjustment, cold and warmth, dryness and damp; pattern, the month setting the pattern; disease and remedy, a disease with its cure.
2. Own party and other party — what generates and helps me is the own party and makes me strong; what I generate, what I check and what checks me is the other party and makes me weak.
3. Season and weight — a strong season is not the same as weight; a strong self is not thereby rich or honoured.
4. The four useful gods — support and check, checking the strong and supporting the weak; adjustment, fire in winter and water in summer; bridging, a middle phase between two; disease and remedy, for the chart with a disease is the precious one.
5. Season, ground and company — the gift of heaven, the state in the month; the gift of earth, being rooted; the gift of men, the help that comes; judged together.
6. The five gods — useful, adverse, favourable, hostile, idle; background only.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: naming a school

Which school takes the *Qiongtong Baojian* as its chief text and reads by cold, warmth, dryness and damp?

- A. Strength and support
- B. Pattern
- C. Seasonal adjustment
- D. Disease and remedy

???+ "Answer"
    C. **Seasonal adjustment.** The *Qiongtong Baojian* reads by the nature of the stems and the climate together. Xin metal born in the zi month, water cold and metal chill, wants bing fire to adjust it.

### Exercise 2: the two parties

Which of these groups belongs to the own party?

- A. the seal, the peer, rob wealth
- B. eating god, wounding officer, direct wealth, indirect wealth
- C. direct officer, seven killings, eating god, wounding officer
- D. direct wealth, indirect wealth, direct officer, seven killings

???+ "Answer"
    A. **The own party, which makes me strong**: what generates me, the seal, and what helps me, the peer and rob wealth. **The other party, which makes me weak**: what I generate, eating and wounding; what I check, wealth; what checks me, officer and killing.

### Exercise 3: in season

In which months are jia and yi wood in season?

- A. hai and zi
- B. yin and mao
- C. si and wu
- D. shen and you

???+ "Answer"
    B. **In season**: the day stem at lin-guan or its peak in the month, or at birth for a yang stem. Jia and yi wood are in season in yin and mao; in hai and zi they are strong, being fed.

### Exercise 4: choosing a useful god

A person born in winter, metal cold and water chill: which kind of useful god does the chart call for?

- A. the god of support and check
- B. the god of adjustment, fire
- C. the god that bridges
- D. the god of disease and remedy

???+ "Answer"
    B. **The god of adjustment.** Born in winter, water in force and the qi cold, the chart wants **fire** to warm it and thaw it. Born in summer, fire in force and the qi dry, it wants **water** above all.

### Exercise 5: a strong self or a weak one

The day stem is at its peak in the day branch, so the day stem is in force, but eating and wounding and the wealth star are stronger still. What is the reading?

- A. a strong self
- B. a weak self
- C. balanced
- D. a following pattern

???+ "Answer"
    B. **A weak self.** Strong is not weight: the day stem is in force, at its peak, but eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing are stronger than it, and the self is weak. Weight is relative, and what it weighs is the day stem against the other gods.

### Exercise 6: all three together

Bing fire as the day stem, born in the wu month, si in the day branch, ding showing in the hour stem, zi in the year branch. Judge season, ground and company, and whether the self is strong or weak.

???+ "Answer"
    Season: in season, bing born in the wu month and wu is bing’s peak, the month in force. Ground: rooted, si in the day branch is bing’s lin-guan, a root in the chief qi. Company: with company, ding fire showing in the hour stem, a peer lending help. Altogether a strong self: three of three. The zi water in the year branch is bing’s fetal stage and an officer checking the self, but one root in the year branch cannot turn the force of the whole, which is the very illustration of strong season not being weight: with more officer and killing to check the self it might be weak.
