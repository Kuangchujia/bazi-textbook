# Lesson 13 · Study, Career and Marriage: the Ten Gods in Practice

[简体中文](/textbook/L13/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L13/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L13/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L13/)

## 1. Study

> The gods at the centre: the seal as the root, the officer star as the discipline, eating and wounding as the talent.

### 1.1 Which star does what

**The seal is the root of study.** It governs papers, qualifications, certificates, schools: the first look at study is at the seal. A seal with ground under it and learning is accomplished; a seal broken by wealth and study is blocked.

**The direct officer is the rope of discipline.** It governs examinations, systems, rules. Officer and seal generating one another — discipline on one side, a foundation of learning on the other — is the best arrangement there is for sitting examinations.

**The eating god is the outflow of talent.** It governs the capacity to learn and a gift for technique. An eating god with qi takes in new knowledge mildly and learns without strain.

**The wounding officer is the star of genius.** It governs original thinking and refusing the usual mould. Wearing the seal, genius with breeding, and strong in academic invention; uncontrolled, cleverness defeating itself.

### 1.2 Good and bad in study

**Good** — officer and seal generating one another, the best for examinations; a seal with qi, a gift for reading; the eating god generating wealth, technique cashed; the wounding officer wearing the seal, academic invention.

**Bad** — wealth ruining the seal, one of the great conflicts of this lesson: an early romance and play, no heart for study, results unsteady; the way out is an officer star to bridge, or the peers taking the wealth to shield the seal. The owl taking the food, the capacity to learn damaged. The wounding officer uncontrolled, clever and unable to settle.

**Neutral** — a strong seal with a strong self: step by step, short on flexibility.

### 1.3 The frame for reading study

First, the seal: is there one? Rooted or not? Rooted, and study has a foundation.

Second, wealth and seal: is the wealth ruining the seal? Can an officer star bridge them?

Third, eating and wounding: eating and wounding with no seal to check them is clever and unable to settle; a seal with no eating or wounding is rigid and without talent.

> A case: a strong seal using xin metal in you as the direct officer — officer and seal both strong; the man graduated from a key university and rose to a departmental rank.

## 2. Career

> The gods at the centre: the direct officer as the frame, wealth to feed a life, eating and wounding as the talent, the seven killings as the handle of power.

### 2.1 Which star does what

**The direct officer is the root of a career.** It governs office and authority, law and regulation, administration: the first look at a career is at the direct officer. With an officer, take the officer first; with none, then take the useful god.

**The seven killings are the handle of power.** Controlled they are authority — the eating god controlling or the seal transforming — and become a keen tool of career. Uncontrolled they are calamity.

**The wealth star is what feeds a life.** Wealth generates the officer: wealth turned into power, wealth and officer both fine.

**The eating god cashes talent.** Generating wealth, technique and speech turned into income; controlling the killing, the killing turned into authority.

**The wounding officer is the star of craft.** Generating wealth, ideas turned into money; wearing the seal, talent with breeding.

### 2.2 Good and bad in career

**Good** — wealth, officer and seal together, the highest arrangement, three chains of energy at once; wealth and officer both fine, money and power feeding each other; officer and seal both whole, qualification and post making each other; the eating god controlling the killing, the killing turned into authority.

**Bad** — the wounding officer meeting the officer, another of the great conflicts of this lesson: lawsuits and prison, a career destroyed. The seven killings uncontrolled, attacking the body. Wealth banding with the killing, lawsuits over money or over women. Officer and killing mixed, honest and crooked together, where the first business is to take out the muddy and keep the clean.

### 2.3 Fields of work at a glance

| Field | Gods at the centre | Key combination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil service, the state | direct officer, seal | officer and seal generating one another |
| Police, military, law | seven killings, lu and blade | eating god controlling the killing, or killing and blade both showing |
| Enterprise and trade | eating god, wealth, seven killings | eating god generating wealth, or controlling the killing |
| Technique, engineering | eating god | eating god generating wealth |
| Art, ideas | wounding officer | wounding officer generating wealth or wearing the seal |
| Sales, speaking | eating god, wounding officer | eating and wounding generating wealth |
| Scholarship, research | seal, eating and wounding | officer and seal generating one another, or wounding officer wearing the seal |

> A case: a gui water day stem born in the xu month, wu earth as the direct officer showing in the month, wealth, officer and seal all strong together and no seven killings mixed in: the highest arrangement of the direct officer, with three chains of energy running at once.

## 3. Marriage

> The gods at the centre: the direct officer in a woman’s chart, the wealth star in a man’s. Purity is the good sign, mixture the bad.

### 3.1 Marriage in a woman’s chart

**The direct officer is the husband.** A pure direct officer and the marriage is steady; a damaged officer star and it is troubled. Officer and seal generating one another: a responsible husband, approved by the elders.

**The seven killings are the lover.** The killing stands for the side attachment. Officer and killing mixed, honest and crooked together, and the affections are in disorder.

**The wounding officer is the star that checks the husband.** The wounding officer meeting the officer is the first prohibition for a woman’s marriage. Where the wounding officer is strong and uncontrolled, the first reading is that it checks the husband.

**The quick list for a woman’s chart:**

- a pure direct officer, good: the husband star clean, the marriage steady.
- officer and seal generating one another, good: a responsible husband, approved by the elders.
- the wounding officer meeting the officer, bad: it checks the husband, the marriage troubled.
- officer and killing mixed, bad: the affections in disorder, a third party easily.

### 3.2 Marriage in a man’s chart

**The wealth star is the wife.** Direct wealth is the wife, indirect wealth the lover. Wealth with a source and the marriage goes smoothly; wealth robbed and it is unsteady.

**The peers are the star that robs the wife.** The peers robbing wealth is the first prohibition for a man’s marriage. Where the peers are strong, marriage is hard to make or comes apart.

**The quick list for a man’s chart:**

- wealth with a source, good: a good wife, a steady marriage.
- wealth generating the officer to guard it, good: no fear of the peers robbing it.
- the peers robbing wealth, bad: a third party comes in, money lost and the marriage damaged.
- wealth banding with the killing, bad: disputes over money or over women.

### 3.3 Conflicts common to both

Wealth ruining the seal: money or the elders’ opposition weighing on the marriage, the nearer the wedding the likelier the trouble, often ending in divorce over the elders’ opposition or a money dispute.

Officer and killing mixed: the proper and the side attachment both present, the affections in disorder. The first business is to take out the muddy and keep the clean.

> A case: a strong wounding officer with no control, checking the direct officer. The woman’s marriage is poor and the couple are strained. With a wealth star to bridge — the wounding officer generating wealth, the wealth generating the officer — it can be resolved.

## 4. The lesson in view

### 4.1 Three fields, one law

| Field | First look | Chief conflict | Key to resolving it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Study | the seal | wealth ruining the seal | an officer star to bridge |
| Career | the direct officer | the wounding officer meeting the officer | the seal checking the wounding officer, or wealth to bridge |
| Marriage | the spouse star | the wounding officer meeting the officer in a woman’s chart, the peers robbing wealth in a man’s | wealth to bridge, or the officer guarding the wealth |

### 4.2 One body many faces in practice

In one chart, switching the field switches the ruling god. A seal broken by wealth in the same chart is bad read for study, and read for career turns on whether the officer star has force: one chart, different conclusions because the field is different. This is the core application of one body many faces in the practical reading of the ten gods.

> A note by Chujia Kuang. Having finished the lesson, take your own chart and walk it through: how does the seal stand for study? how does the direct officer stand for career? how does the spouse star stand for marriage? Walk the three fields and you have a view of the whole chart. Reading a chart is not reciting formulae; it is learning to switch the field and look at the same chart again.

## Summary of the lesson

1. Study: the seal as the root, officer and seal generating one another the best for examinations, wealth ruining the seal the chief conflict.
2. Career: the direct officer as the frame, wealth, officer and seal together the highest arrangement, the wounding officer meeting the officer the chief conflict.
3. Marriage: the direct officer in a woman’s chart and the wealth star in a man’s; purity the good sign, mixture the bad.
4. One body many faces: one chart read field by field gives different conclusions, and that is the core of practice.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: reading study

Which god is looked at first in reading study?

???+ "Answer"
    **The seal.** The seal governs papers, qualifications, certificates and schools. A seal with ground under it and study is accomplished; a seal broken by wealth and study is blocked. The best combination is officer and seal generating one another.

### Exercise 2: conflicts in career

What are the two greatest conflicts in the field of career, and how are they resolved?

???+ "Answer"
    First, the wounding officer meeting the officer: the wounding officer checks the direct officer, lawsuits and prison. The way out is the seal checking the wounding officer, or wealth to bridge — the wounding officer generating wealth, the wealth generating the officer. Second, the seven killings uncontrolled, attacking the body. The way out is the eating god controlling the killing, or the seal transforming it.

### Exercise 3: reading marriage

What does a woman’s chart and a man’s chart each take as the first measure of marriage?

???+ "Answer"
    A woman’s chart takes the **direct officer** as the husband star: pure and the marriage is steady, meeting the wounding officer and it is troubled, officer and killing mixed and the affections are in disorder. A man’s chart takes the **wealth star** as the wife star: with a source the marriage goes smoothly, robbed by the peers it is unsteady, banded with the killing it is damaged over money or over women.
