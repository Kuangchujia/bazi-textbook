# Lesson 9 · The Images of the Ten Gods

[简体中文](/textbook/L09/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L09/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L09/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L09/)

## 1. The direct officer

> The root of honour, checking me with goodwill: noble when wealth feeds it and the seal shields it, disaster when it is injured or bound away. The direct officer is the first of the ten and the root of honour; the work of a chart is to keep a good direct officer in good order.
> **From the *Sanming Tonghui*:** Wan Minying says that the direct officer is yin and yang matched and so useful in its control, and that is how it fulfils its way. It heads the six patterns, and only one is wanted; more is not fitting. The *Yuanyuan Fu* calls it the name of loyalty and respect, the title of ordering the state and the household. The *Wanqi Fu* calls the officer star the master that honours the person and the source of stipend: meeting wealth it comes out easily, meeting the blade it is left stranded.

### 1.1 The definition

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Relation | what checks me at the opposite polarity: yin and yang differ, and the check carries rule and goodwill |
| Example | a jia wood day stem meeting xin metal, yin metal checking yang wood |
| Function | control with goodwill, as people obey a government and its laws. The chart takes the direct officer as the root of honour: social standing and the standing that comes with authority |

### 1.2 Its faces

In brief: help the direct officer and its good side shows; damage it and trouble with the law, pressure and petty men follow.

| Persons | patrons and superiors: with the officer of use, help and recognition come readily |  |  |
| Kin | children in a man’s chart, since the direct wealth generates it; the husband in a woman’s | too many officer and killing in a woman’s chart and marriage is seldom smooth, often repeated; too weak in a man’s and the tie to children is thin |  |
| Matters | work, post, power; where the officer is undamaged by food and wounding and not bound away, government or public institution | strong wealth feeding the officer likes investment; the officer generating the seal and shielding it draws patrons |  |
| Temperament | capacity to manage, capacity to take an opening | pressure, obstruction, vanity |  |
| Work | administration, enterprise |  |  |

### 1.3 Reading it

| State | Judgment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| of use | help and recognition readily, work goes well | the officer has wealth feeding it and the seal shielding it, the pattern clean |
| injured | food and wounding damage it or it is bound away; bad for work | the wounding officer meeting the officer brings a hundred troubles |
| too strong | vanity, comparison, great pressure | too much officer turns to killing: control with goodwill becomes pressure without it |

> The point to hold. The direct officer is the hinge of the whole reading. Whether a person can hold his place in the world is asked first of the officer: is there one, in which pillar, is wealth feeding it, is the seal shielding it. A clean officer and the chart is clean; a confused officer and it is confused.
> The core rules from the *Sanming Tonghui*. Meeting the officer, look for the seal: a direct officer pattern most likes to move into the seal, and the seal shielding the officer is the first requirement. An officer without a seal is solitary; a seal without an officer is empty. Where the officer star appears repeatedly it is argued as a killing and no longer as a fortunate god, and must be controlled to be of use. The hour is where things come to rest: it can restrain an officer too strong and reinforce one growing weak, and fortune or ill turns on it. Each phase gives a different officer: metal as officer, office stern and clear, judgment in penal matters; wood as officer, rank honourable, cautious and mild; water as officer, modest and well liked, rising step by step; fire as officer, fierce, given to punishment, now rising and now falling; earth as officer, steady and hard to offend, with laws clearly applied.
> The songs of the *Sanming Tonghui*: the straight officer star is taken from the month branch, and likes wealth and seal to come in the year and hour; untouched by breaking, harm, clash or void, it brings wealth and honour together. The officer star mostly wants a strong self; a weak self must seek the quarter where qi is strong, with the seal running alongside and wealth in a strong place, and no clash or injury, and then there is honour. The direct officer mostly wants purity and mildness, with the four pillars uninjured, and then the examination comes; at the hour it likes wealth strong, and in the pillars it is glad to see much seal.
> The *Xiangxin Fu*: the officer star is kindly and mild, its noble air high; it holds plenty and is generous and large, open-hearted with a voice steady and clear, handsome in bearing and quick and bright in temper.

## 2. The seven killings

> The killer that attacks the body, like sleeping with a tiger: controlled by food or transformed by the seal it becomes authority, and with neither it is disaster. The old name is the killer, first of the unlucky gods, whose business is to attack the body. Controlled it is the indirect officer; uncontrolled, the seven killings. Controlled, its good side shows; helped, its bad.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** Shen Xiaozhan says the killer attacks the body and hardly looks a good thing, and yet the greatly honoured patterns mostly hold seven killings. Controlled to measure, the killer is used by me: like a great hero, hard to drive and yet, handled with method, capable of shaking heaven and earth in a moment. That is why kings and generals so often have one. He adds that using food to control the killer is the best: a strong killer, strong food and a healthy self make a pattern of the highest honour.

| Function | to attack the body. The killer dares to think and dares to act, going forward and counting the cost after; it stands for the rough and the rash, and rashness leads easily to trouble with the law |

### 2.3 Controlled and uncontrolled

| --- | --- | --- |

> The point to hold. The seven killings are a tiger, and the first question is not whether there is one but whether it is held. The eating god to control it, the seal to transform it, the wounding officer to bind it: all are means. The heroes of old often had one, and the whole thing is control and transformation.
> Three ways of handling it, from the *Sanming Tonghui*. The eating god controlling the killing, the hard way: one killer raising trouble can be taken by one hand, so a single eating god holds a single killing, and this is the first method. The seal transforming the killing, the soft way: many killers running together are dissolved by one benevolence, so one seal amidst mixed killings turns force into kindness and an enemy into a friend. The wounding officer binding the killing: wit against force, yielding with steel inside.

## 3. Direct and indirect wealth

> The source that feeds a life, to be carried by a strong self: direct wealth steady and indirect wealth restless, kept it stays whole and shown it is divided. Wealth feeds a life, everyone wants it and not everyone gets it. Direct and indirect are both good things and are counted as fortunate gods.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** Shen Xiaozhan says wealth is what I check, the thing I use, and because it can generate the officer it is good. Money and goods, wife and concubine, capacity, the post horse: all these are wealth. Wealth likes a deep root and should not be too much on show. A strong self that does not let the wounding officer through and does not mix the seven killings makes a noble pattern.

### 3.1 Direct against indirect

| Item | Direct wealth | Indirect wealth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relation | what I check at the opposite polarity: yin and yang differ, and the name is proper | what I check at the same polarity: alike, and the thing is accidental |
| Example | jia wood meeting ji earth | jia wood meeting wu earth |
| Kind of income | lawful and steady: salary, fixed income, low-risk investment | irregular: windfalls, speculation, high-risk investment, money of unclear origin |
| Kin for a man | the wife | a lover or concubine |
| Character | steady and lasting | quick to come and go, gathering and scattering |

### 3.2 Its faces

Wealth in general: it feeds a life. A man strong in wealth usually has an artistic gift and a certain style. Wealth and seal are a pair of opposites: the seal is conservative and keeps the old, wealth is inventive and adaptable.

A man strong in wealth has income from several quarters | a strong wealth star with a weak self is a burden rather than a gain |  |

### 3.3 Reading it

| State | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| of use | steady income, money flowing, a good wife in a man’s chart |
| strong and checking the seal | attractive and money-minded but not studious; bad for study and name |
| direct and indirect together | repeated marriage or a woman outside in a man’s chart; income from several quarters |
| a taboo | burdened by money, money coming and going and never gathering |

> The point to hold. Direct and indirect alike feed a life; the first question is whether it can be kept, not how much can be made. Wealth and seal are a standing contradiction: study and the seal, or money and invention. Where the two fight in a chart, the man spends his life choosing.
> The core rules from the *Sanming Tonghui*. One horse in the stable and no one dares chase it; one horse in the open and everyone chases it. Wealth in its place, stored and not shown, cannot be taken; wealth on show, floating on the stems, is fought over. Wealth should be kept and not shown: kept it is ample, shown it is easily broken. Strong wealth with a declining self dies as soon as it is supported: where wealth is too strong and the self too weak, a wealth cycle or further support for wealth can put the man’s life at risk, the extreme of the rich house and the poor man, where wealth is not fortune but harm. A direct officer with a seal is not as good as one mounted: the officer matched with the seal is well enough, but not so direct and strong as the officer fed by wealth, the horse. The seal shielding the officer is holding, wealth feeding him is attacking, and the best is to have both.

## 4. The seal

> The mother that generates the self, shelter and benefit: the direct seal is the ground of honour in letters, and the indirect seal that meets food turns its face and becomes the owl. The seal is authority, and it is the parent generating the self, the sign of everything that benefits me. Direct and indirect are both called the seal. The direct seal is generally a fortunate god; the indirect meeting the eating god is unlucky and is called the owl.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** Shen Xiaozhan says the seal is valued for generating the self, and direct and indirect make patterns equally good, so wealth and seal are not divided into direct and indirect but argued as one pattern. The patterns differ: a seal with the officer showing above is noble where the officer star is clean; a seal using food and wounding to drain its excellence is for a strong self with a strong seal, where the fear is excess and the drain brings out the fine; a seal in quantity using wealth is for a heavy seal and a strong self, where wealth shows above to hold back the excess.

In general: the seal generates the self and is the source of my qi, as a parent gives life. Draining officer and killing, holding off the wounding officer, blunting the food: these are its functions.

> The point to hold. The seal is the instrument of authority: the officer star is standing and the seal is the handle. An officer without a seal is a name without substance; a seal without an officer is substance without a name. Both together make the noble pattern.

## 5. The eating god

> The star of longevity and fortune, the first control on the killing: talent generating wealth, mild and unhurried; taken by the owl, fortune and years both suffer. The eating god is the natural enemy of the seven killings, and its business is to control them. Hence the star of longevity: whether a life is long is often read from the state of the eating god. It is generally a fortunate god.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** Shen Xiaozhan says the eating god drains qi by nature and is liked because it can generate direct wealth, so the eating god generating wealth is a fine pattern. The patterns differ: generating wealth, with a strong self, strong food and wealth showing above, is greatly honoured; going to the killing and the seal is the most commanding; the metal-water eating god using the killing is noble and fine. He adds that food kept and wounding shown makes a man hard in temper: by whether food and wounding are kept or shown, hardness and softness can be judged.

| Example | a jia wood day stem meeting bing fire, yang wood generating yang fire |
| Function | the natural enemy of the seven killings, whose business is to control them. The eating god controlling the killing is called good overcoming evil: the day stem comes through unharmed |

### 5.2 Its faces

In general: the eating god is the star of longevity and of fortune. In good state, health and long life; meeting punishment, clash, check or robbery, injury and accident, and in the gravest cases an early end or trouble with the law. A man of the eating god is mild by nature, sympathetic and imaginative, good at expressing himself; strong and uncontrolled, he thinks much and does little, and talks a good game.

> The point to hold. The eating god is a star of fortune, and fortune has levels. Controlling the killing is the highest: the killing held and ill turned to good. Generating wealth is the middle: talent turned into money. Draining the self is the base: knowing how to enjoy life. Health is always read from it: clashed or checked, the star of longevity is damaged and health will show it.

## 6. The wounding officer

> The star of skill, the ruin of the officer: clever and rebellious, noble with the seal; wounded to the end and meeting the officer, a hundred troubles. The wounding officer is the natural enemy of the direct officer and its business is to damage him. The wounding officer meeting the officer brings a hundred troubles, and it is generally counted an unlucky god. It is also the star of skill: controlled and transformed, the talent is abundant.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** Shen Xiaozhan says the wounding officer is not a fortunate god and is in fact the fine qi, which is why men of letters are mostly found in wounding officer patterns; and summer wood meeting water, winter metal meeting fire, are the finest of the fine. The pattern changes a great deal: generating wealth turns ill to good, carrying the seal controls the wounding and makes it noble, using the officer suits metal and water alone. The gist: examine the season, measure the strength, weigh what is liked and disliked, look at purity and mixture, finer than fine, and not to be held to one rule.

> The point to hold. The wounding officer is the double-edged star of the ten: talent and danger together. The first question is whether it is controlled or transformed: the seal to control it gives talent a root, wealth to transform it turns it into money. The second is whether the direct officer is in the chart: meeting the officer is the great taboo, but where wealth bridges the two, the wounding officer generating wealth and wealth generating the officer, a wealthy and noble pattern can come of it. The third is where it stands: in the year the family fortunes scatter, in the month the brothers suffer, in the day husband and wife disagree, in the hour the children are unruly. Men of the wounding officer are often the most inventive people about, and the easiest to give offence. Learning to control it is learning to turn rebellion into something of one’s own.

## 7. The same-rank

> Brothers helping, and brothers dividing: precious to a weak self, a burden to a strong one. The same-rank is the bond of brothers and stands for cooperation, sharing, competition and contention; it does not necessarily mean loss. A strong day master generally makes it an unlucky god, but not always.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*:** on lu established in the month and the rob wealth of the month, Shen Xiaozhan says lu is the lu hall met in the month, and lu is rob wealth, so the two make one pattern and need not be separated; both take wealth, officer, killing or food showing in the stems as their use. The lu-and-rob pattern must find wealth or officer for its use: with the officer, it wants wealth and seal along with it and cannot stand on a lone officer; with wealth, it must carry food and wounding to transform the rob and generate the wealth; with the killing, the killing must be controlled. He adds that a lu-and-rob pattern with no wealth or officer but using wounding and food, draining its excess, is also fine qi; and that spring wood and autumn metal using it are noble, since wood meeting fire is bright and metal generating water is quick.

| Function | taking wealth and dividing fortune. A strong day master generally makes it an unlucky god, but it also stands for cooperation and help, and is not to be judged in one way |

### 7.2 Its faces

In general: the same-rank is the bond of brothers and stands for social ties and the strength of a team. The peers stand for people and for subordinates, and many a great entrepreneur has strong peers, since they are the hands, the staff and the employees; without them little gets done. Too strong, though, and money disputes damage the partnership.

> The point to hold. Of the ten, the same-rank is the one that most depends on whether the self is strong or weak. To a strong self it is flowers on brocade, and too many flowers make a muddle; to a weak self it is fuel in the snow, and the more fuel the warmer. The second question is whether wealth stands beside it: the peers taking wealth is the first sign of loss, but where officer or killing controls the peers and guards the wealth, it becomes a team making money together. The third is the pillar: in the year the family had brothers, in the month there are many, in the day husband and wife compete, in the hour the children have many friends. The place settles the closeness. Great entrepreneurs often have strong peers not because their fate is good but because the peers are staff and subordinates, and one man alone does not build anything large.

## 8. The rob wealth

> First of the takers, worse than the same-rank: controlled, a team does the work; uncontrolled, blades and loss. The rob wealth is the natural enemy of the wealth star and its business is to take wealth, hence the name, and hence also the chase-the-horse. A strong day master generally makes it an unlucky god, but not always.
> **From the *Ziping Zhenquan*, on the yang blade**, which is rob wealth at its worst: Shen Xiaozhan says the yang blade is the god that robs my direct wealth, the seven killings of the direct wealth. It stands one place after lu and only the five yang stems have it, which is why it is the yang blade. It is called a blade and not a rob because the robbing is extreme. The blade should be held down, and officer or killing both suit, with wealth and seal along, which is noble and conspicuous. With the officer, a blade showing above is no worry, since the officer controls it; with the killing showing, a blade above achieves nothing, since the blade binds the killing and the merit is lost. With wealth, the pattern does not like it; but where wealth has a deep root and food and wounding are used to turn the blade into generating wealth, riches may still come.

### 8.1 The definition

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Relation | what is like me at the opposite polarity |
| Example | a jia wood day stem meeting yi wood, yang wood meeting yin wood |
| Function | the natural enemy of the wealth star, whose business is to take wealth. It takes wealth, fortune and life: whatever can be taken, it takes |

### 8.2 Its faces

In general: rob wealth means robbing and seizing wealth, hence the chase-the-horse. The classic says that meeting the rob with no wealth about, even out of a wealth year, still means loss, which shows how fierce it is. It usually stands for blades, weapons, surgery and disaster. With the wounding officer it is called turning the back on lu and chasing the horse, and Luolu Zi says that turning the back on lu and chasing the horse keeps a man in poverty and misery. The horse is noble qi, the officer star, lu and the wealth star: rob wealth and wounding officer together and both wealth and officer are lost.

### 8.3 Reading it

| State | Judgment | Why |

> The point to hold. The rob wealth and the same-rank both help the self and take wealth, and the rob is fiercer: the same-rank divides, the rob seizes. Division can be discussed; seizure leaves nothing. The classic on meeting the rob with no wealth about, still losing, shows how bad it is. Three questions: is there wealth beside it, since with wealth it takes and without there is no great harm for now; is there officer or killing to hold the rob and guard the wealth, which turns ill to good; has it met the wounding officer, since the two together turn the back on lu and chase the horse, losing money and place at once, which is a bad combination. It also stands for blades and surgery, and a year that clashes or checks it calls for care against injury.
> The core rules on the peers from the *Sanming Tonghui*. Power and blade met again by power and blade, and a man dies of blade or medicine: where authority and the yang blade overlap and a cycle brings more of the same, there is disaster by steel or poison, the worst showing of rob wealth. Brothers break the wealth and the wealth is of use: the same-rank takes wealth and the wealth can still be used, which is the team making money. The peers do not always take: a weak self helped by them finds patrons. Husband and wife injured as soon as married, because the same-rank lies hidden under the horse: the same-rank hidden beneath the wife-and-wealth star makes marriages repeated and unstable, and a man heavy in peers should watch for a change of marriage.

## The five classical conflicts

> With the eight images read, these five are looked at again in the whole. Behind every fear there is a way out, and inside every bad pattern a door to authority. Taken together from the lesson:

**1. The owl taking the food**, the indirect seal checking the eating god: the gravest.

The indirect seal meeting the eating god turns its face and becomes the owl, and the fortunate god checked loses three capacities at once: of thought, since inspiration and expression go; of earning, since food no longer generates wealth and the source is cut; and of resistance to illness, since food no longer controls the killing and the killing comes at the body. The eating god punished, clashed, checked or robbed means talent held down and thought running dry at the least, and injury, lawsuits or prison at the worst. A woman strong in seal checking food and wounding has no children, or miscarries, or a thin tie to them. An eating god too strong with no wealth, though never taken, is often selfish and mean, a point to add to the section on the eating god. The way out: use wealth to injure the seal and guard the food. Where owl and food face each other, bring in wealth and the owl turns to check the wealth instead, and the eating god is saved: wealth breaking the seal to guard the food.

**2. The peers taking wealth**, the commonest.

Wealth feeds a life and is the wife star in a man’s chart. The peers divide it, partners take their share, loss comes and partnerships suffer; a man has trouble marrying or his marriage changes. Rob wealth stands for blades, weapons, surgery and disaster: it takes wealth, fortune and life, whatever can be taken. With the wounding officer it is turning the back on lu and chasing the horse, and Luolu Zi says that keeps a man in poverty. The same-rank also takes wealth and also stands for cooperation, sharing and competition, and does not necessarily mean loss: a weak self helped by the peers finds patrons, which is the central point of the section on the same-rank. The ways out: food and wounding to transform the peers and generate wealth, or the officer star to control the peers and guard the wealth. The final test is the strength of the day master: a strong self robbed is a disaster, a weak self helped is a fortune.

**3. The eating god controlling the killing**, ill turned to good.

The seven killings head the unlucky gods, and attacking the body they bring maiming, illness and prison, like sleeping with a tiger. The eating god is their natural enemy and its business is to hold them, so the day stem comes through, which is why it is called the star of longevity. Whether a life is long is often read from the state of the eating god, the first point of the section on food. A chart where the eating god controls the killing comes through danger and turns it into authority and fortune. A man using the killing this way suits the army, police and law, administration, or enterprise. In a woman’s chart the eating god is the star of fortune, and controlling the killing well keeps her safe and benefits husband and children.

**4. The wounding officer meeting the officer**, a hundred troubles.

The direct officer heads the ten and is the root of honour, and the wounding officer leans on its strength and lets the day stem out beyond reason and law. With the two of equal force trouble never ends: lawsuits, disputes, petty men, a changing career and strained relations. And the wounding officer has another face: thought, gift, inspiration, artistic talent, leaving the straight road, playing the unconventional hand, taking the short cut. The old saying runs that the five controls of the wounding officer bring a hundred troubles and that the wounding officer that benefits things is clever beyond the common, which is the dialectic of the section. The ways out: wound it to the end, since half-wounded it lets the officer come in strength and the harm is past telling; carry the seal to control it, so that learning restrains talent; or generate wealth to transform it, turning rebellion into commercial value.

**5. Wealth injuring the seal**, gain at the cost of right.

The seal is name, study and mother, and stands for the conservative, the old, the step by step, good for study and short on flexibility. Wealth is invention, adaptation, present gain, artistic temper and the enjoyment of life. The two are a natural contradiction: strong wealth checking the seal gives a head for money and no heart for study, and much wealth makes no scholar; a strong seal with no wealth gives learning and high principle and no knack for a living. Direct wealth strong checks the mother and thins the tie to her, which crosses the section on the seal with the section on wealth. The way out: the officer star as the bridge, wealth generating the officer and the officer generating the seal, so that wealth does not injure the seal and officer and seal generate one another into a noble pattern: the best example of bridging at the level of the ten gods.

> The five in brief: the owl taking the food is the gravest, since the fortunate god is checked; the eating god controlling the killing is the best, since the unlucky god is held; the peers taking wealth is the commonest, seen every day. The way out of each lies inside the rules of generation and check: wealth to injure the seal and guard the food against the owl; food and wounding, or the officer, against the peers; the food as the general against the killing; the seal as a yoke and wealth as a channel against the wounding officer meeting the officer; the officer as a bridge against wealth injuring the seal.
> The beginner should fix the first three, the owl taking the food, the eating god controlling the killing and the peers taking wealth, and take the rest as practice brings them. The three lessons on combinations, 10 to 12, set out the ways out one by one.

## Summary of the lesson

1. Direct officer: what checks me at the opposite polarity, a mild restraint, standing for justice and responsibility; the husband star in a woman’s chart, children in a man’s.
2. Seven killings: what checks me at the same polarity, a harsh restraint, standing for decision and force; controlled it is the indirect officer, uncontrolled the seven killings.
3. Wealth, direct and indirect: the source that feeds a life, and the two are not divided in value; direct for steady income, indirect for windfalls; the wife and the lover in a man’s chart, the father in either.
4. The seal: authority generating me, direct and indirect; the direct for orthodox learning, the indirect for the side disciplines; meeting the eating god it becomes the owl and takes the food.
5. Eating god: what I generate at the same polarity, mild talent, cheerful; the star of longevity and the enemy of the killing; the daughter in a woman’s chart and the grandparents in either.
6. Wounding officer: what I generate at the opposite polarity, rebellious talent, clever and unruly; the enemy of the officer; the son in a woman’s chart.
7. Same-rank: like me at the same polarity, cooperation and competition, confident and opinionated; brothers and friends; it helps and it takes, and the strength of the self decides which.
8. Rob wealth: like me at the opposite polarity, seizing and taking, impulsive and quarrelsome; the enemy of wealth, taking wealth, fortune and life, also called the chase-the-horse; with the wounding officer it turns the back on lu.
9. The five conflicts: the owl taking the food, the gravest; the peers taking wealth, the commonest; the eating god controlling the killing, the best; the wounding officer meeting the officer; wealth injuring the seal. The way out of each lies in the rules of generation and check.
