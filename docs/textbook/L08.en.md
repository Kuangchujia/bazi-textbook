# Lesson 8 · Setting Out the Ten Gods

[简体中文](/textbook/L08/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L08/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L08/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L08/)

## 1. Same polarity and different polarity

### 1.1 The definition

With the day stem at the centre, the polarity of any other stem against it decides whether the god is direct or indirect:

| Relation | Definition | Example, with jia wood as the day stem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| same polarity | the same yin or yang as the day stem | jia meeting bing: both yang |
| different polarity | the opposite polarity to the day stem | jia meeting yi: jia yang, yi yin |

> The principle: the ten gods divide into direct and indirect on polarity alone. Same polarity gives the indirect, opposite polarity the direct.

### 1.2 Three things learned in practice

> A note by Chujia Kuang. The danger for a beginner is theory without practice. Twenty years of it have left me three hard rules.
> **One: settle the polarity first, derive the god after.** Meeting a stem, the first question is not which god it is but whether it is the same polarity as the day stem or the opposite. Once that is settled the direct and indirect divide at once, faster than any table.
> **Two: learn your own chart first.** Take it as the answer key. If you are a jia day stem, geng is your seven killings and xin your direct officer. Fix your own ten gods and every other day stem is a matter of putting yourself in its place.
> **Three: the relation matters more than the name.** The names can be learned slowly, but the five relations have to be reflex: what generates me, what is like me, what I generate, what I check, what checks me. Beginners who rush at the names and skip the relations are the ones whose readings go wrong.

## 2. What the day stem is

In this art the stem of the day pillar stands for the person whose chart it is. It is called the day stem, or the day master.

How the other seven characters stand to it, and how they combine with it, is what shows temper, capacity, and the direction a working life takes.

> An example: ren-xu year, gui-chou month, ren-shen day, geng-shen hour. The day pillar is ren-shen, so ren is the day master, and every ten god is derived from ren.

## 3. The five relations

### 3.1 The five

With the day master at the centre, generation and check give five classes. From the *Yuanhai Ziping*:

> 克我者，正官偏官。生我者，正印偏印。我克者，正财偏财。我生者，伤官食神。同我者，劫财败财。
> What checks me is the direct or indirect officer; what generates me the direct or indirect seal; what I check the direct or indirect wealth; what I generate the wounding officer and eating god; what is like me rob wealth and the wastrel.

**What generates me, the seal.** The phase that feeds me: for a ren water day master, metal. It governs parents, teachers and patrons.

**What is like me, the peer.** The same phase: for ren water, water. It governs brothers, friends and colleagues.

**What I generate, food and wounding.** The phase I feed: for ren water, wood. It governs children, students and subordinates.

**What I check, wealth.** The phase I control: for ren water, fire. It governs wife and money, and what comes under one’s charge.

**What checks me, officer and killing.** The phase that controls me: for ren water, earth. It governs office, superiors and pressure.

Within each relation the polarity divides direct from indirect: opposite polarity gives the direct, same polarity the indirect. The *Yuanhai Ziping* says that the direct officer is yin and yang matched and so useful in its control, while the indirect officer is seven places away and so checks by force, which is why it is called the seven killings.

From the *Yuanhai Ziping*, on how the ten shift with the day stem, taking jia as the example:

> 甲见甲为比肩，乙为劫财；丙为食神，丁为伤官；戊为偏财，己为正财；庚为偏官（七杀），辛为正官；壬为倒食（偏印），癸为印绶（正印）。
> Jia meeting jia is the same-rank; yi, rob wealth; bing, the eating god; ding, the wounding officer; wu, indirect wealth; ji, direct wealth; geng, the indirect officer or seven killings; xin, the direct officer; ren, the inverted food or indirect seal; gui, the seal or direct seal.

The heart of it: take jia as the standard and the ten stems answer to the ten gods in order, from jia the same-rank through to gui the direct seal. Change the day stem and the positions stay, while the gods shift. Jia’s list is the master key to the whole shifting.

## 4. Deriving each of the ten

Five relations, each divided by polarity, give ten. Each is given below with its classical statement and its derivation, using jia, yang wood, and yi, yin wood, as the two day stems.

**What generates me: the seal.** A seal is a covering and a cord is what one receives. The direct seal is a kind mother, mild and feeding, asking nothing back; the indirect seal, also called the owl, is a strict teacher who also supports, but on terms.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping* on the seal: what generates me is the seal. An officer without a seal is not a true officer; a seal without an officer becomes a fortune in itself. The man of the seal is thoughtful, seldom ill through life, well fed and provided for, and enjoys wealth and stipend ready made.

| Day stem | Indirect seal, same polarity | Direct seal, opposite polarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia, yang wood | ren water | gui water |
| yi, yin wood | gui water | ren water |

**What is like me: the peer.** The same-rank is a brother, same qi and one branch, sharing sweet and bitter; rob wealth is a competitor, friend and enemy at once, and shows what it is when money is on the table.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping* on the peers: the same-rank is the bond of brothers, like qi seeking like; rob wealth is the waster of wealth, and checks the father and the wife.

| Day stem | Same-rank, same polarity | Rob wealth, opposite polarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia, yang wood | jia wood | yi wood |
| yi, yin wood | yi wood | jia wood |

**What I generate: food and wounding.** The eating god is a thin steady stream, mild with staying power, talent coming out of itself; the wounding officer is a spring bursting up, fierce and outward, the edge showing.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping* on food and wounding: the eating god is also called the star of longevity and the star of rank. The wounding officer must be wounded to the end; if it is not, and the officer comes in strength, the harm is past telling.

| Day stem | Eating god, same polarity | Wounding officer, opposite polarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia, yang wood | bing fire | ding fire |
| yi, yin wood | ding fire | bing fire |

**What I check: wealth.** Direct wealth is salary, steady and lasting, a long thin stream; indirect wealth is a windfall, quick to come and quick to go, high peaks and deep troughs.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping* on wealth: what I check is wife and wealth. Direct wealth is what I buy with my own spirit and strength; indirect wealth is the wealth of the crowd, wealth in circulation.

| Day stem | Indirect wealth, same polarity | Direct wealth, opposite polarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia, yang wood | wu earth | ji earth |
| yi, yin wood | ji earth | wu earth |

**What checks me: officer and killing.** The direct officer is law and measure, orderly and mild, a restraint that protects; the seven killings are a tiger, harsh and fierce, turning on the man who holds them the moment the grip slips.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping* on the indirect officer: a man with one sleeps with a tiger. Its majesty may overawe the herd, but lose the guard for a moment and it will bite. Controlled, it is the indirect officer; uncontrolled, the seven killings.

A tiger handled well is of use and handled badly is a danger of one’s own making. Of the ten, this is the one most in need of control and transformation.

| Day stem | Seven killings, same polarity | Direct officer, opposite polarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jia, yang wood | geng metal | xin metal |
| yi, yin wood | xin metal | geng metal |

> With these five, and the derivations for jia and yi, the other eight stems follow of themselves.

> The mnemonic: what generates me is the direct seal, the indirect seal and the owl; what is like me, the same-rank and rob wealth; what I generate, the eating god and the wounding officer; what I check, direct and indirect wealth; what checks me, the direct officer and the seven killings.

## 5. How the ten stand to one another

### 5.1 Generation among them

The ten generate one another in a single chain:

The seal generates the peers, so elders support the self and the root is fed. The peers generate food and wounding, so one’s own force turns into output. Food and wounding generate wealth, so talent turns into money. Wealth generates officer and killing, so money turns into place and power. Officer and killing generate the seal, so pressure comes back as learning and cultivation.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping*: an officer without a seal is not a true officer, and a seal without an officer is a fortune in itself. Officer and killing generating the seal is the step where pressure becomes cultivation. Generation between the same polarity is strong, between opposite polarity weak.

### 5.2 Check among them

Check among the ten gives the five standing conflicts of a chart, and each is the clue to where the trouble lies:

Officer and killing check the peers: power pressing on a competitor. The peers check wealth: brothers and friends dividing the money, the sign of loss. Wealth checks the seal: greed for money damaging a name, and bad for study. The seal checks food and wounding: talent held down, and not good for children. Food and wounding check officer and killing: talent defying authority and drawing trouble.

> From the *Yuanhai Ziping*, on the way of checking: rob wealth is the waster of wealth and checks the father and the wife, so the peers check the wealth star and brothers and friends take a share. Wealth can injure the seal, so greed damages the name and gain costs right. The indirect seal takes the food, which harms children: talent held down and few children. The wounding officer meeting the officer brings a hundred kinds of trouble: talent offending superiors. The seven killings attack the body, and are precious only when controlled: uncontrolled they strike, controlled they become authority.
> Check between the same polarity is strong and the two cannot stand together; between opposite polarity it is weaker and can be reconciled. How these five are resolved is set out in lesson 9, on the five classical conflicts.

> A summary from Chujia Kuang. The ten gods are the language of a chart. The day stem is me, and how the other seven characters stand to me is the ten gods. What generates me is the seal, what I generate is food and wounding, what checks me is officer and killing, what I check is wealth, what is like me is the peer. Five sentences and the ten are fixed; learn them to the point of reflex.

## Summary of the lesson

1. Same and different polarity: yang stems are yang and yin stems yin; the same as the day stem gives the indirect, the opposite the direct.
2. The day stem: the stem of the day pillar, standing for the person, and the centre of every relation.
3. Five relations: what generates me, the seal; what is like me, the peer; what I generate, food and wounding; what I check, wealth; what checks me, officer and killing.
4. Deriving them: the five relations divided by polarity give ten.
5. How they stand: the round seal, peer, food and wounding, wealth, officer and killing, and back to the seal; and the five conflicts, the owl taking the food, the peer taking wealth, the eating god controlling the killing, the wounding officer meeting the officer, and wealth injuring the seal.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: polarity

With jia wood as the day stem, which stem is of different polarity?

1. jia
2. bing
3. geng
4. yi

???+ "Answer"
    D. Jia is yang, and jia, bing, wu, geng and ren are the same polarity; yi, ding, ji, xin and gui are the opposite. Yi is yin wood, opposite to yang jia.

### Exercise 2: which god

With jia wood as the day stem, what is geng metal?

1. direct officer
2. seven killings
3. direct seal
4. indirect wealth

???+ "Answer"
    B. Jia is yang wood; geng is yang metal, and metal checks wood, so this is what checks me at the same polarity: the seven killings. Jia meeting xin, opposite polarity, is the direct officer; meeting ren, the same polarity generating me, the indirect seal; meeting gui, the direct seal.

### Exercise 3: wealth injuring the seal

Which line describes wealth injuring the seal?

1. the seal checks food and wounding, holding talent down
2. food and wounding check officer and killing, drawing trouble
3. wealth checks the seal: greed for money damages the name
4. the peers check wealth: brothers divide the money

???+ "Answer"
    C. Wealth stands for money and things; the seal for learning, name and mother. Strong wealth checking the seal means greed damaging the name, poor study, and a mother alienated or at odds.

### Exercise 4: the peer taking wealth

What does the peer taking wealth usually mean in a chart?

1. money flowing in without end
2. being dragged down by friends and brothers into loss
3. a smooth career
4. good health

???+ "Answer"
    B. The peers are brothers, friends and colleagues; wealth is money and the wife. The peers taking wealth means friends dividing it: being pulled into loss by friends, losing money in partnership, and money that will not stay. Where it is heavy in a chart, be careful of investments, of partnerships and of lending.
