# Lesson 17 · Reading the Luck Cycles and the Years

[简体中文](/textbook/L17/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L17/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L17/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L17/)

## 1. The chart, the luck and the year

The closing lines of the *Jishan Pian*:

> Look first at the month as the outline, then read good and ill from the day. The three parts must make a pattern, and the four pillars want to see wealth and officer.

Seventeen lessons done, and back to the beginning: the month as the outline, the day stem as master, the pattern as the thing that matters. That is the whole of the art.

### 1.1 What each of the three is

| Element | What it is | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The chart | the ground one is born with | the conditions fixed at birth |
| The luck cycle | the trend of ten years | the stages of fortune met afterwards |
| The year | the change within a year | the good and ill of each particular year |

> The core: how high the chart stands, and how the fortune runs, is judged from the luck cycle and the year together. The chart is the body, the luck and the years are its use.

### 1.2 How the three stand to one another

- The luck is the outline, the year the detail: the luck governs the trend of ten years, the year works out the changes of each one.
- The luck sets the key: is the luck favourable to the useful god of the chart?
- The year settles the particulars: how the year and the luck act on each other.

## 2. Judging a luck cycle

### 2.1 Stem and branch within a cycle

Each ten-year cycle is a pair, one stem and one branch:

| Stretch | The stem leads | The branch leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first five years | 70% | 30% |
| The last five years | 30% | 70% |

### 2.2 The test

- Generating and supporting the useful god: a good cycle, the run of things goes with you.
- Checking and consuming the useful god: a bad cycle, the run of things is blocked.

Points to watch:

- In the first five years weigh the stem, in the last five the branch.
- Generating and supporting the useful god is good; checking and consuming it is bad.
- A good cycle is not good throughout: the years still tell.
- A bad cycle is not bad throughout: the years still tell.

## 3. Judging the year with the luck

### 3.1 The formula

The good or ill of a year is never read alone; it is read with the luck cycle it falls in.

- A good year with the luck supporting it: the good is increased.
- A good year with the luck holding it back: the good is reduced.
- A bad year with the luck supporting it: the ill is increased.
- A bad year with the luck holding it back: the ill is reduced.

### 3.2 How the luck and the year meet the chart

- Combination: the five combinations of stems, the six of branches, the three-phase combinations — look at what the combination turns into and how it stands to the useful god.
- Clash: the six clashes of branches, the image of movement and impact.
- Punishment: the punishments among branches, standing for lawsuits, wounds and illness.
- Harm: the harms among branches, damage in secret, trouble from inside.

In practice, combination, clash, punishment and harm are read with the god concerned and the pillar they fall in before good or ill is called.

## 4. Using the luck and the year: worked cases

> From lesson 8: the four cases below run through the combinations that suit and do not suit all ten gods.

### 4.1 The officer using wealth, wealth using the officer, the officer using the seal

Lessons 10 and 11 set out what suits each god in combination: the direct officer likes wealth to feed it and the seal to shield it, the wealth star likes the officer to guard it and eating to generate it, and so on. At the level of the luck and the year one more factor comes in, the strength of the self: the same combination runs in opposite directions for a strong self and a weak one.

The table gathers the wealth and officer combinations under each state of the self, as a quick reference for reading a cycle:

| Case | Cycles that suit | Cycles that do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A weak self | the seal, the peer, rob wealth, which generate and help | officer and killing, wealth, which check and consume |
| A strong self | officer and killing, wealth, which check and consume | the seal, the peer, rob wealth, which generate and help |
| The officer star damaged | the seal controlling the wounding officer, shielding the officer | eating and wounding: the wounding officer meeting the officer |
| Wealth robbed | officer and killing guarding the wealth, eating and wounding generating it | the peer and rob wealth, which take it |

The logic is not complicated: what is weak is made up, what is strong is drawn off, and the combinations follow what lessons 10 and 11 set out. Learn this table beside the verse for judging a cycle and it goes in fastest.

### 4.2 Eating god generating wealth, wounding officer generating wealth

Eating and wounding generating wealth is a pattern of riches: money made by talent and craft. At the level of the luck, what such a chart most fears is a cycle of the seal, the owl taking the food, and a cycle of the peers, robbing the wealth, for these two strike directly at the two supports of the pattern: they cut the source that generates the wealth and block the road by which it comes out.

| Case | Cycles that suit | Cycles that do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A strong self | eating and wounding, wealth, which drain and consume | the peer, rob wealth, the seal, which generate and help |
| A weak self | the peer, rob wealth, the seal, which generate and help | eating and wounding, which drain the self too far |
| The eating god taken by the owl | wealth controlling the owl, shielding the eating god | the seal: the owl taking the food |

Note the row where the eating god is taken by the owl. The eating god is the star of longevity and the source of wealth; taken by the owl, at the least money is lost, and at the worst health suffers. The only way out is wealth checking the owl and shielding the eating god. That is why a chart of eating and wounding generating wealth does so well in a wealth cycle: the wealth not only brings money of its own but protects the machine that makes it.

### 4.3 Lu and blade using the officer, using the killing

| Case | What it needs | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lu and rob using the killing | the eating god must show, to control the killing | the eating god controls the killing, and then the lu and blade |
| The yang blade using the killing | eating and wounding must not show, for they damage the killing | the blade is violent, and the killing must keep its force |

> For the worked case of a yang blade using the killing, see the chapter on controlling the four unlucky in lesson 12; it is not set out again here.

### 4.4 The verse for judging a cycle

> A weak self does well in seal and peer cycles; a strong self in officer and wealth cycles.
> The officer star damaged wants the seal; wealth robbed wants the officer star.
> The owl taking the food wants wealth to break it; the wounding officer meeting the officer, eating and wounding are what to avoid.
> Wealth banding with the seven killings wants eating to control it; lu and blade using the killing want wealth to help it.

Points to watch:

- A cycle is read by the strength of the self: the weak are supported, the strong are checked.
- It is read by the combination of gods: the officer using wealth, wealth using the officer, the eating god generating wealth.
- It is read by lu, blade and the killing: lu and blade using officer and killing, the killing using the eating god to control it.
- It is read with the chart: what the chart has, the cycle supplies.

## 5. In closing

> Chujia Kuang, at the end of the course. Seventeen lessons, all done. What these years have taught me most: this art is not learned, it is practised. The book is only a starting point, and the real work is in the reading done day by day.
> Four things to hold: one who knows the changes does not need to divine — understanding the principle matters more than reckoning the number. One body many faces — the same chart, another angle, is another life. Fullness brings loss, modesty brings gain — aim at lin-guan, not at the peak. All things meet by cause and condition — keep what comes, let go what goes.

## A note on the limits of what is said here

Two statements in this lesson are conditional readings in the traditional art, not predictions: the punishment standing for lawsuits, wounds and illness in 3.2, and the eating god taken by the owl bringing trouble to health in 4.2. Both must be judged with the gods of the chart, the pillar they fall in and the luck and year as a whole. Neither is a settled forecast about any person or any state of health, and neither takes the place of medical or legal advice.

## Summary of the lesson

1. The three together — the chart is the ground one is born with, the luck the ten-year stage, the year the change within a year.
2. The chart is the ground, the luck and the year the use. The luck is the outline, the year the detail.
3. Judging a cycle — generating and supporting the useful god is a good cycle, checking and consuming it a bad one; the stem leads the first five years, the branch the last five.
4. The year with the luck — a good year with a good luck increases the good, a good year with a bad luck reduces it; a bad year with a good luck increases the ill, a bad year with a bad luck reduces it.
5. Using the two — by the strength of the self, by the combination of gods, by lu, blade and killing; a weak self supported, a strong self checked.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: the three together

How do the chart, the luck cycle and the year stand to one another?

- A. the luck decides the chart, the year decides the luck
- B. the chart is the ground one is born with, the luck the ten-year stage, the year the change within a year
- C. the year is the ground one is born with, the chart the ten-year stage, the luck the change within a year
- D. the three have nothing to do with one another

???+ "Answer"
    B. **The chart is the ground one is born with, the luck the ten-year stage, the year the change within a year.**

### Exercise 2: judging a cycle

What is the test of a luck cycle?

- A. the phases of its stem and branch
- B. whether it generates and supports the useful god, or checks and consumes it
- C. whether its stem and branch are of the same phase
- D. whether it is rooted in the chart

???+ "Answer"
    B. **Generating and supporting the useful god makes a good cycle; checking and consuming it makes a bad one.**

### Exercise 3: the year with the luck

A good year falling in a good luck cycle: what is the reading?

- A. the good is increased
- B. the good is reduced
- C. the ill is increased
- D. the ill is reduced

???+ "Answer"
    A. **A good year with a good luck increases the good.** A good year with a bad luck reduces it; a bad year with a good luck increases the ill; a bad year with a bad luck reduces it.
