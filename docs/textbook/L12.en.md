# Lesson 12 · Combinations (III): the Four Unlucky Controlled

[简体中文](/textbook/L12/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L12/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L12/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L12/)

## 1. The wounding officer, and what suits it

> From the *Siyan Dubu*, on the wounding officer: the wounding officer meeting the officer is the great taboo of the pattern; where the useful god is not harmed, why fear the officer coming. From the *Jishan Pian*: the wounding officer meeting the officer’s luck again, and disaster comes unlooked for. — The wounding officer put to use, talent overflows; meeting the officer, trouble comes from every side.

### 1.1 Its faces

The wounding officer is the star of technique, what I generate of the opposite polarity, and its first business is to control the direct officer. Unlike the eating god, which is mild and orthodox, the wounding officer is rebellious and pushing: the most double-edged of the ten. Overflowing with talent it is a leaping flame; uncontrolled it is a wild horse. Among persons it stands for the son in a woman’s chart and for the grandparents; in the mind, for technical skill, writing and speech; in health, for the mouth and tongue and the organs of generation; in place, for the stage, the place of entertainment, the studio of ideas. Yang Xiu of the Three Kingdoms had wit to spare and showed every edge of it, crossed Cao Cao’s forbidding again and again, and was put to death: the classic picture of a wounding officer with no control, meeting the officer and coming to harm.

> A note by Chujia Kuang. The wounding officer is the thorniest of the ten: the brilliant are mostly strong in it, and so are those who never stop making trouble. The same wounding officer, wearing a seal, is an uncommon talent; meeting the officer, it is a scourge. The first question to ask of a wounding officer: who is standing beside it?

### 1.2 What suits it

**Generating wealth.** The wounding officer is the original spirit of the wealth star: talent cashed, a career taking off. With a wounding officer generating wealth, once the luck comes right the break-out is very strong.

> A case: this chart’s wounding officer generates wealth. The man was top of his class and class monitor at primary school; in 2019 his career leapt and he went from sales manager to third in the company.

**Wearing the seal.** The seal checks the violence of the wounding officer and keeps the fineness of its talent: violence turned to nobility, manners with ability.

> A case: this chart’s wounding officer wears the seal, and the man is courteous, his parents comfortably off. A wounding officer wearing the seal is the type of talent that has been taught manners.

### 1.3 What does not

**The wounding officer meeting the officer.** The wounding officer checks the direct officer and leaves him without power: trouble from every side, lawsuits and prison, quarrels and illness.

> A case: the jia-wu luck from 2012 and the wu-xu year of 2018; the wounding officer grew greatly and the direct officer was damaged, and the man went to prison.

**The wounding officer uncontrolled.** No seal to check it, no wealth to draw it off: the heart higher than heaven, the fate thinner than paper, ideas in plenty and not one of them landed.

### 1.4 The quick list

- generating wealth, good: talent cashed, a career taking off.
- wearing the seal, good: violence turned to nobility, manners with ability.
- meeting the officer, bad: lawsuits and prison, trouble from every side.
- uncontrolled, bad: the heart higher than heaven, the fate thinner than paper.

## 2. Lu and the blade, and what suits them

> From the *Xiji Pian*, on lu and the blade: day lu returning to the hour with no officer star is called the road to the blue clouds. Rob wealth and the yang blade, avoid meeting them in the hour; with the year and the luck arriving together, disaster comes at once. From the *Jishan Pian*: the yang blade clashing or combining with the year lord, and calamity bursts out. — Lu in the hour unbroken by an officer is the most fortunate of arrangements. The blade is a tiger: uncontrolled it mauls.

### 2.1 Its faces

Lu and the blade are the seven killings of wealth, what robs wealth and divides fortune. They differ from the seven killings: the killings attack the body and want your life, lu and blade take wealth and want your money. Among persons, brothers and sisters, colleagues, rivals, partners; in health, the hands and feet and limbs, the motor system; in place, the arena, the sports ground, the factory floor. Lu is the lin-guan stage, the blade is a yang stem at its peak, month rob is a yin stem at its peak, and their force runs blade, then lu, then rob. The basic definitions are in lesson 10. Xiang Yu, the hegemon of Chu, could lift a cauldron and was unmatched in fierceness, but his yang blade was too strong and no officer or killing controlled it, and he cut his own throat at the Wu River: the blade is a tiger, and uncontrolled it mauls others and itself.

> A note by Chujia Kuang. Lu and blade robbing wealth is among the commonest events in daily reading. Meeting them, ask three things first: is there an officer or killing to govern them? Can it govern? And when it cannot, in which field of life does the loss fall?

### 2.2 What suits them

**Using an officer or killing to control them.** Lu and rob using the killing need the eating god shown, to control the killing and then control the blade; the yang blade using the killing must not show eating or wounding, which would damage the killing — the blade is violent, and the killing must keep its force to control it. Killing and blade both showing, and the rank is that of a prince.

> A case: wu earth born in the wu month is a yang blade, needing jia as the killing to control it and gui as wealth to feed the killing. A xin wounding officer showing in the stem checks the killing to no purpose and lowers the pattern.

**Using eating and wounding to generate wealth.** Lu and rob turned through eating and wounding reach a higher level; the yang blade turned the same way reaches a lower one. Raw strength made into a craft and a trade.

> A case: a jian-lu chart using gui as the eating god to generate wealth; the man holds a master’s degree, works as a trade intermediary, and is now head of the European region for an investment company in Hong Kong, China.

### 2.3 What does not

**Lu and blade uncontrolled.** With the officer and killing absent the peers rob the wealth: loss of money, financial disputes. The yang blade is the graver case, and to damage the killing with eating or wounding is to break the very tool that controls the blade.

> A case: the zi-wu clash of 2014, the peers robbing wealth, and the man was cheated by a classmate.

### 2.4 The quick list

- using the killing, good: killing and blade both showing, a rank like a prince.
- generating wealth, middling to good: a high level for lu and rob, a lower one for the yang blade.
- the peers robbing wealth, bad: loss of money, financial disputes.

## 3. The seven killings, and what suits them

> From the *Siyan Dubu*, on the killing: pattern by pattern, weigh it out, and the killing weighs heaviest; turn the killing into authority, and why fear harm to the useful god. From the *Xiji Pian*: if the hour meets the seven killings, seeing them is not necessarily ill; with the month controlling and the stem strong, the killing turns instead into authority and seal. Four pillars of pure killing with control: certainly a rank of the first grade. — The seven killings controlled turn into authority; uncontrolled they are the source of calamity.

### 3.1 Its faces

The seven killings are the natural enemy of the day stem, what attacks the body, what bears me ill will. Among persons, petty men and enemies, the son in a man’s chart and the lover in a woman’s; in the mind, drive, boldness, authority; in health, sudden illness, wounds and surgery; in place, the prison, the police office, the barracks. The seven killings are a tiger slept beside: handled well it serves you, handled badly it mauls you. Cao Cao killed and decided without hesitation, held the emperor and ordered the lords, and through a life of campaigns kept his killing controlled and ruled the north; yet his suspicion ran deep and he killed in his sleep, which is the dark side of a killing uncontrolled.

> A note by Chujia Kuang. The seven killings test the reader more than any other star: tell controlled from uncontrolled, and honour and meanness are settled at once. The eating god controlling is the hard killing; the seal transforming is the soft one. Controlled, authority over the court; uncontrolled, calamity at the door.

### 3.2 What suits it

**The eating god controlling the killing.** The eating god is the natural enemy of the seven killings and is called the star of longevity. It checks the violence of the killing directly and turns the killing into authority: the hard method, direct and effective.

> A case: the eating god controls the killing; the man is the son of a wealthy family, holds an MBA from a leading Japanese university, and works in the family firm.

**The seal transforming the killing.** The seal dissolves the violence of the killing: killing and seal generating one another, and rank and name come through. Wit turning violence: the soft method.

**The seven killings uncontrolled.** A fierce god with no one to govern it: it attacks the body, work fails, health suffers. The base text sets this line at the end of this section, where it does not belong; it is given again under what does not suit the killing, in 3.3.

### 3.3 What does not

**Wealth banding with the killing.** Wealth feeding the killing to attack the body, arming the enemy with money: mostly lawsuits and prison, quarrels and illness. Wealth banding with the killing heads the three prohibitions.

> A case: bing as wealth and wu as the killing, the banding breaking the pattern; divorce, and a career that will not go.

**The seven killings uncontrolled.** A fierce god uncontrolled is the same as wealth banding with the killing: it attacks the body, and work fails.

**Officer and killing mixed.** The direct officer and the seven killings seen together: clear and dark not separated, pulled both ways. The first business is to take out the muddy and keep the clean.

> A case: officer and killing mixed and the killing uncontrolled, and the marriage is poor.

### 3.4 The quick list

- the eating god controlling, good: the killing turned to authority, the hard method.
- the seal transforming, good: killing and seal generating one another, the soft method.
- wealth banding with the killing, bad: arming the enemy with money, first of the three prohibitions.
- the killing uncontrolled, bad: it attacks the body, calamity.
- officer and killing mixed, bad: take out the muddy and keep the clean.

## 4. The lesson in view: the four unlucky turned round

### 4.1 The way of turning them round

The common law of the four unlucky: control them, transform them, turn the ill into authority. It differs from the four fortunate, which are used with the flow: the fortunate are helped, the unlucky are handled. The wounding officer wears the seal or generates wealth; lu and blade are controlled by officer and killing or turned by eating and wounding; the seven killings are controlled by the eating god or transformed by the seal. The death of Yang Xiu, a wounding officer uncontrolled; the fall of Xiang Yu, a yang blade uncontrolled; the hegemony of Cao Cao, a seven killings controlled — three histories, one argument: the unlucky are not to be feared for existing, but for having no one to govern them.

### 4.2 The three side by side

- Wounding officer — the star of technique: generating wealth or wearing the seal is good, meeting the officer or running uncontrolled is bad. The wounding officer is talent: uncontrolled it is wild, controlled it is fine.
- Lu and blade — they rob wealth and divide fortune: controlled by officer and killing it is noble, turned by eating and wounding it is rich, uncontrolled it robs wealth and breaks the house. The blade is a tiger, and controlled it becomes a mount.
- Seven killings — the killing that attacks the body: controlled by the eating god or transformed by the seal it is authority, banded with wealth or uncontrolled it is calamity. The killing is a stallion, and tamed it runs a thousand li.

### 4.3 The frame for reading the four

First, control and transformation: is there a god to control it? Is that god truly rooted or floating? With control, authority; without, calamity.

Second, root: an unlucky god rooted shows its violence plainly, and a controlling god rooted controls in earnest.

Third, combination: is the controlling god itself checked? Where a yang blade uses the killing and eating or wounding shows, the killing that controls the blade is damaged and the pattern falls of itself.

> The way of the four unlucky lies not in the gods but in whether you can handle them. The same seven killings with an eating god is authority over the court, with a wealth star calamity at the door: one substance, different combinations, different conclusions.
> A note by Chujia Kuang. Having finished the four, take your own chart and set it beside them: is the wounding officer controlled? do officer and killing govern the lu and blade? is the seven killings transformed by the eating god or the seal? The unlucky are not to be feared for existing, but for having no one to govern them. An unlucky god with a governor is the sharpest knife you own.

## Summary of the lesson

1. Wounding officer: the star of technique; it likes generating wealth and wearing the seal, and dislikes meeting the officer and running uncontrolled; the wounding officer meeting the officer is trouble from every side.
2. Lu and blade: they rob wealth and divide fortune; they like being controlled by officer and killing and turned by eating and wounding, and dislike running uncontrolled, robbing wealth and breaking the house.
3. Seven killings: the killing that attacks the body; it likes the eating god’s control and the seal’s transformation, and dislikes wealth banding with it, running uncontrolled, and officer and killing mixed.
4. One body many faces: an unlucky god controlled is authority, uncontrolled is calamity, and all of it lies in control and transformation.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: the wounding officer

What arrangement does the wounding officer most like, and what does it most fear?

???+ "Answer"
    It most likes generating wealth, talent cashed, and wearing the seal, violence turned to nobility. It most fears meeting the officer, lawsuits and prison, and running with no control and no wealth, talent that never lands.

### Exercise 2: controlling the blade

When a yang blade uses the killing, why must eating and wounding not show?

???+ "Answer"
    The yang blade is violent and needs the force of the seven killings to control it. To damage the killing with eating or wounding is to break the tool that controls the blade: a weakened killing cannot control the blade, and the pattern falls of itself. Lu and rob are different, and may show the eating god to control the killing and then the blade.

### Exercise 3: the three prohibitions

What are the three great prohibitions of the seven killings?

???+ "Answer"
    Wealth banding with the killing, arming the enemy with money, first of the three; the killing uncontrolled, attacking the body; officer and killing mixed, honest and crooked together, where the first business is to take out the muddy and keep the clean.
