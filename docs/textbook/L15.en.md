# Lesson 15 · Putting Strength to Work

[简体中文](/textbook/L15/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L15/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L15/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L15/)

## 1. The three standards for judging strength

From the *Siyan Dubu*, on strength:

> The five phases in generation and in force need not fear clash or punishment; east, west, south and north, the count runs out and then it is done.

The strong need not fear checking or draining, the weak fear clash and checking. Once strength is judged, what matters is what suits: the strong want checking, by control, drain or consumption; the weak want support, by generation or help.

> From lesson 8: the generating and checking of the ten gods is the working basis of what suits and what does not. The strong are handled by check and drain, the officer or killing checking, eating and wounding draining, wealth consuming; the weak by generation and help, the seal generating, the peers helping. The four core prohibitions: the officer fears the wounding officer, wealth fears the rob, the seal fears wealth, the eating god fears the owl. Meeting any of the four, look closely for a control or a transformation.

### 1.1 The methods in current use

Four methods of judging strength are in circulation:

- Counting the phases: strength settled by how much generates and helps the day stem. Simple and direct, good for a quick reading, but apt to miss the real difference in force between stems and branches.
- The month as the centre, with ground and company: the month as the core, judged together with being rooted and with help. This is the mainstream method and the one this course uses.
- Scoring: points added and taken away for season, ground and generation or check, strength made a number. The virtue is standardisation; the fault is that it over-simplifies the relations between stems and branches and loses the finer information in a chart.
- Strength charts: a table of the ten stems through the months, or a chart of phase content by month, looked up to settle strength. Direct and convenient, but bound to a fixed template, short on flexibility and depth. The representative system is the strength chart in Qiu Pingce’s *Pingce Mingli Mizong*.

### 1.2 The method this course uses

This course uses the month as the centre, with ground and company, in two steps: strength first, weight after.

Step one: settle the state of the day stem in the month.

| Polarity | Strong states | Weak states |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A yang stem (jia, bing, wu, geng, ren) | birth, lin-guan, peak | none of the three |
| A yin stem (yi, ding, ji, xin, gui) | lin-guan, peak — birth does not count for a yin stem | neither of the two |

> A yang stem in any one of birth, lin-guan or peak and the day stem is strong; a yin stem at lin-guan or its peak and it is strong. A strong day stem is the ground on which wealth and officer can be carried; with the day stem out of season, the force of wealth and officer has nothing to stand on.

Step two: compare the amount of force.

Compare how many lin-guan and peak states stand on the side of the day stem, its lu and blade, against eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing, and settle the weight of the day stem from that.

| What is compared | How | The test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The force of the own party | the number of lin-guan and peak states among seal and peers | many and with force: the day stem is not weak |
| The force of the other party | the number of lin-guan and peak states among eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing | many and with force: the day stem is pressed |
| Both together | own party against other party | own party prevailing: a strong self; other party prevailing: a weak self |

> A case: geng as the day stem with shen, its lu, in the chart, and the day stem is strong. Jia as wealth generates ding as the officer, and wealth generating the officer is noble: the man has the regard of those above him.

### 1.3 Points to watch

| Point | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The month first | the month is the first ground for judging strength | lesson 14, section 5 |
| Then root and help | ground and company settle the weight | lesson 14, section 5 |
| A strong seal is not a strong self | a strong seal with an unrooted day stem is still a weak self | lesson 14, 3.4 |
| A strong season is not weight | in season but the other party prevailing gives a weak self | lesson 14, 3.3 |

## 2. Judging a strong self

### 2.1 The strongest

- In season: the day stem at lin-guan or its peak in the month.
- Strong many times over: lin-guan or peak again in other branches.

> Case 1, the strongest: a professor of media. Wu earth as the day stem, at its peak in the year and hour branches, the chief qi of xu in the day branch, peers throughout the chart, and so the strongest.
> Case 2, the strongest: a business family. A strong self taking the benefit of wealth and officer, and the family firm is of great size.

### 2.2 Strong, in two shapes

- In season: the day stem at lin-guan or its peak in the month.
- With company: the stem’s own qi showing again among the stems.

> Case 3, strong, in season and with company: jia as the day stem, at its peak, a yang blade, in the month and so in season; at lin-guan in the yin year branch and so rooted. Altogether strong.
> Case 4, strong, in season and with company: wu earth with two wu blades, controlled by jia as the killing. A strong self takes the good of wealth and killing, and help from the opposite sex comes easily; but the xin wounding officer checking the killing is one check too many, and so the career is ordinary.

- Out of season: the month is not a strong ground for the day stem.
- Rooted: lin-guan or peak in another branch.
- With company: peers and seal in the stems in number, helping and generating.

> Case 5, strong, out of season but rooted and with company: a niece. Ding fire as the day stem, in the chou month at the store, a resting state; but si in the day branch is its peak and wu in the hour branch its lin-guan, so it is rooted. Ding as a peer and bing as a rob show in the stems, peers throughout the chart and to good effect, so it has company. Altogether strong.

### 2.3 Points to watch

| Type | Condition | Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The strongest | in season and strong many times over | force beyond measure, read as a following pattern |
| Strong | in season with company, or out of season but rooted and with company | judge the whole, and do not hold to one test |
| A strong self is not necessarily rich | strong with no wealth or officer | wealth and officer must still fit |
| A strong self wants checking | the strong self as the disease | check it with officer and killing, eating and wounding, wealth |

## 3. Judging a weak self

### 3.1 The weakest

The weakest is a day stem taking no force from the month and bearing, across the chart, a great weight of check, drain and consumption, so that it stands at an absolute disadvantage in the balance of force.

- Out of season: the day stem resting, sick, dead or cut off in the month.
- Much checking and draining: what checks me and what I generate stronger than the day stem.

The weakest is not a bad fate. Where the other party is extremely strong and the day stem follows it, a following pattern, great wealth and honour may come of it. Both cases below are among the weakest and both prospered.

> Case 6, the weakest: a former student, very rich. Very rich, though the day stem is extremely weak, a pattern following wealth and officer.
> Case 7, the weakest: following wealth and killing, made rich in construction. Yin, wu and xu form a wealth combination and band with wu as the killing, a chart following wealth and killing. From 1991 the wealth kept banding with the killing, and he made his money in construction work.

### 3.2 Weak, in two shapes

- In season: the day stem takes force from the month.
- The other party strong: but eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing stronger still.

> Case 8, in season and weak: a sole trader. Ren born in the zi month, a yang blade pattern, the day stem strong. But wu takes one chen and two xu as chief qi, stronger than the day stem, and so the self is weak. The seven killings want the eating god to control them, and the geng seal checking the eating god is one check too many: the man is easily framed by others.
> Case 9, in season and weak: a teacher. Seal throughout the chart, but too much wood and the fire goes out, and so the self is still weak.

- Out of season: the month is not a strong ground for the day stem.
- The other party strong: eating and wounding, wealth, officer and killing stronger than the day stem.

> Case 10, out of season and weak: a state-owned enterprise. Yi wood as the day stem, at birth in the wu month, but birth does not count for a yin stem, and so the day stem is out of season. Out of season it cannot carry wealth and officer, and is burdened by wealth, and by officer in marriage, career and authority.
> Case 11, out of season and weak: a colleague. Xin metal as the day stem with no force anywhere in the branches, and so a weak self. Wealth breaks the seal and bands with the killing, and the marriage is at a distance and the career poor, the relation with those above him strained.
> Case 12, out of season and weak: a trader. Gui water at its peak in hai, and the day stem strong. But bing as wealth is strong, wealth bands with the killing, and the killing is uncontrolled, so lawsuits and talk come easily.
> Case 13, out of season and weak: yi wood with no force in the branches, and so a weak self. Bing as the wounding officer drains the self, and gui as the seal controlling it is good: with the good of the seal, the household is harmonious and elders and superiors give recognition and help.

### 3.3 Points to watch

| Type | Condition | Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The weakest | out of season with much checking and draining | force extremely weak, read as a following pattern |
| Weak | in season with the other party strong, or out of season with it strong | judge the whole, and do not hold to one test |
| A weak self is not necessarily poor | weak and following | a following pattern may bring great wealth and honour |
| A weak self wants support | the weak self as the disease | generate and help it with seal and peers |

## 4. What suits a strong self

> From the *Zi Ping Zhen Quan*, on support and check: a strong self has no need of the seal, and a weak one does not use the wounding officer. — The strong need no more generation and want checking, draining and consumption; the weak must be supported and must not be checked further.

### 4.1 The strong want checking

Check the strong: bring the day stem back to balance by control, by drain or by consumption.

| Way | God | Effect | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Control | officer and killing, direct and seven | direct pressure, the strongest of the three | first choice where officer or killing is rooted and has force |
| Drain | eating and wounding | the energy led outward, the surplus drawn off | usable where eating or wounding is rooted and has force |
| Consume | wealth, direct and indirect | the force split off, wealth feeding a life | usable where wealth is rooted, or where a strong self meets wealth |

> The order among the three: control before drain, drain before consume. In practice choose by the pattern in hand: with a seven killings in the chart, control; with eating and wounding showing in the stems, drain; with wealth and officer both fine, consume.

### 4.2 The strong do not want support

Already strong, and no more generation: the seal and the peers are what to avoid.

- The seal, which generates me: over-strong, further seal is a burden, as too much wood puts out the fire.
- The peers, which help me: over-strong, further peers are a contest, as a strong self with no check.

### 4.3 Points to watch

| Point | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose among the three | control where officer or killing has force, drain where eating and wounding has force, consume where wealth is rooted | lesson 14, 4.1 |
| Support added to a strong self | the seal coming to generate a strong self adds to it, as too much wood putting out the fire and burning the wood | lesson 14, 3.4 |
| No rule by the label alone | strong is not necessarily rich; wealth and officer must still fit | lesson 14, 3.3 |

> Chujia Kuang, from practice: a strong self wants checking, but not every strong chart is to be checked flat. Control is the hard method; drain and consume are often the surer. Drawing the talent off through eating and wounding is easier for the person to live with than the officer and killing pressing down; consuming through wealth serves the long run better than draining through eating and wounding. The order of choice: first whether there is a control, then whether there can be a drain, and last whether a consumption is needed.

## 5. What suits a weak self

> From the *Ditiansui*, on support and check: what is out of season wants support, what is weak wants generation. — The out-of-season and the weak must be held up, by the seal generating and the peers helping.

### 5.1 The weak want support

Support the weak: give the day stem something to stand on, by generation or by help.

| Way | God | Effect | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Generate | the seal, direct and indirect | the original qi replenished, the root tended | first choice where the day stem is rooted |
| Help | the peers, peer and rob | shoulder to shoulder through the difficulty | the only way out where the day stem is unrooted |

> The order between the two: with a root use the seal, without a root use the peers. Where the day stem is rooted the seal generating it is best, the root being what receives the generation; where it is unrooted no amount of seal can be absorbed, and the peers helping is the only way out.

### 5.2 The weak do not want checking

Already weak, and no more checking: officer and killing, eating and wounding, wealth, are what to avoid.

- Officer and killing, which check me: weak to the limit, further checking presses it down, as much earth burying the metal.
- Eating and wounding, which drain me: weak to the limit, further draining robs it, as much water floating the wood.
- Wealth, which consumes me: weak to the limit, further wealth topples it, as an out-of-season self meeting wealth.

### 5.3 Points to watch

| Point | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose between the two | the seal where the day stem is rooted, the peers where it is unrooted | lesson 14, 3.4 |
| Checking added to a weak self | officer and killing checking a weak self leave it weaker, as much earth burying the metal and sinking it | lesson 14, 3.4 |
| Following is another way of thinking | where the day stem is extremely weak, go with the weakness rather than support it | this lesson, 3.1 |

> Chujia Kuang, from practice: a weak self wants support, but not every weak chart is to be generated flat. First, is the day stem rooted? Rooted, use the seal; unrooted, use the peers. What matters in support is not the amount but the direction. A chart is a boat and the day stem the water: water carries the boat and water overturns it. With the direction right, a little water crosses; with it wrong, a flood goes nowhere.

## 6. From the classics: the Wuyan Dubu, in the Sanming Tonghui, excerpts

> It is the chart with a disease that is precious; without a hurt there is nothing uncommon; where the disease is taken out of the pattern, wealth and rank follow gladly.

What it means:

- A chart with a disease, a flaw, is not thereby bad, and may be the making of a noble pattern.
- What matters is whether the disease can be taken out of the pattern.
- The disease gone, and wealth, office and rank come of themselves.

> With a killing, discuss the killing alone; with no killing, then discuss the useful god. So long as the killing star is taken out, the month need not be feared however strong.

What it means:

- With a seven killings in the chart, the killing is the first business.
- With no seven killings, then look to other useful gods.
- Once the killing is controlled, the month may be as strong as it likes.

## 7. From the classics: the Siyan Dubu, in the Sanming Tonghui, excerpts

> Look first at the month and weigh the pattern out; take the day as master and discuss wealth and officer alone.
> Single and it is easily taken, tangled and it is hard to see; what to keep and what to drop, set out clearly, for the pattern must be weighed with care.
> The day stem high and strong, the month carrying it and in season; wealth taken as the thing, substance and firmness as the rule.
> The outline in use, and clash and punishment are what it most fears; a clash on the luck is slow, a clash on the useful god is ill.
> Pattern by pattern, weigh it out, and the killing weighs heaviest; turn the killing into authority, and why fear harm to the useful god.

What it means:

- Look at the month first, which is the same as this lesson’s rule that the month is the first ground for judging strength.
- Take the day stem as master, and wealth and officer as the focus.
- A single, clean pattern is easily read; a tangled one is hard to see.
- The month in use, and a clash is the worst thing for it.
- Turn the killing into authority: a controlled seven killings becomes power.

## 8. Chujia Kuang, in sum

> The heart of the strength method is not in deciding strong or weak, but in what follows the decision: a strong self wants checking, a weak one wants support. That one word, what suits, is worth a great deal. Force turns with the luck: the adverse god of today may be the useful god of tomorrow, and today’s weakness may become strength in the next luck. The heart of one body many faces is exactly this: never nail a conclusion to a single point in time.

## Summary of the lesson

1. The three standards — the state in the month first, the balance of force second, season, ground and company together.
2. Step one: the state of the day stem in the month, strong or out of season.
3. Step two: the force of the day stem against wealth and officer, weight or lightness.
4. The month is the first ground for judging strength.
5. A strong self — the strongest, in season and strong many times over; strong, in season with company, or out of season but rooted and with company. The strong want checking and do not want support.
6. A weak self — the weakest, out of season with much checking and draining; weak, in season with the other party strong, or out of season with it strong. The weak want support and do not want checking.
7. What suits — a strong self by officer and killing, eating and wounding, wealth; a weak self by seal and peers.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: judging strength

Yi wood, a yin stem, in the wu month, where wu is yi wood’s birth: what state is that?

- A. Out of season, birth not counting for a yin stem
- B. Strong, birth being strong
- C. The strongest
- D. A following pattern

???+ "Answer"
    A. **Birth does not count for a yin stem.** Yi wood at birth in the wu month, but a yin stem is strong only at lin-guan and at its peak, and so the day stem is out of season. Case 10 in the lesson is exactly this: yi born in the wu month, out of season, and burdened by wealth and officer.

### Exercise 2: the strongest

Which of these is the strongest, read as a following pattern?

- A. out of season, rooted and with company
- B. in season, with lin-guan or peak again in other branches
- C. in season but the other party stronger
- D. out of season with much checking and draining

???+ "Answer"
    B. **The strongest**: in season, at lin-guan or the peak in the month, and lin-guan or peak again in other branches.

### Exercise 3: what suits a strong self

Which method suits a person with a strong self?

- A. generating the self with the seal
- B. helping the self with the peers
- C. checking the self with officer and killing
- D. the seal and the peers together

???+ "Answer"
    C. **A strong self wants checking**: control by officer and killing, drain by eating and wounding, consumption by wealth. Generation by the seal and help by the peers are what to avoid.

### Exercise 4: what does not suit a weak self

Which method does not suit a person with a weak self?

- A. generating the self with the seal
- B. helping the self with the peers
- C. checking the self with officer and killing
- D. the seal and the peers together

???+ "Answer"
    C. **A weak self wants support**: generation by the seal, help by the peers. Control by officer and killing, drain by eating and wounding and consumption by wealth are all what to avoid.

### Exercise 5: both together

The day stem is a yang blade, at its peak, in the month, but the seven killings hold the chief qi of three branches and are stronger than the day stem. What is the reading, and what suits?

- A. a strong self, to be checked
- B. a weak self, to be supported
- C. the strongest, following the strong
- D. the weakest, following the weak

???+ "Answer"
    B. The day stem is strong, at its peak, but the seven killings are stronger, and so the self is **weak**, and a weak self wants **support**, the seal generating and the peers helping. Case 8 is the example: ren born in the zi month, a yang blade pattern, but wu holding one chen and two xu as chief qi, stronger than the day stem, and so a weak self.
