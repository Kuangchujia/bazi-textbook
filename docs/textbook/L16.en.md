# Lesson 16 · Spirits and Stars

[简体中文](/textbook/L16/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L16/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L16/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L16/)

## 1. The fortunate stars

The fortunate stars are those that lend a hand in a chart. The first step in learning them is to know the common ones by heart.

### 1.1 The noble of heaven’s yi

The most important of the fortunate stars: it turns ill into good and brings a way through difficulty. It is found from the day stem, and where the branch is there, the noble is there. The verse looks like a nursery rhyme, but known by heart it is a real help in reading a chart.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The verse | jia and wu take the ox and the sheep; yi and ji the rat and the monkey; bing and ding the pig and the fowl; ren and gui the hare and the snake; geng and xin the tiger and the horse. This is where the noble is found. |
| How to find it | take the noble from the day stem; where the branch appears, there it is |
| What it does | the star that turns ill into good: a chart with the noble has help when trouble comes, and rescue when danger comes |

### 1.2 The noble of heaven’s virtue

Found from the month, so that people born in different months have a different noble of virtue. It governs a level life, few illnesses and few troubles, and stands with the noble of the moon’s virtue as a pair of auspicious stars.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | born in the first month, ding; second, shen; third, ren; fourth, xin; fifth, hai; sixth, jia; seventh, gui; eighth, yin; ninth, bing; tenth, yi; eleventh, si; twelfth, geng |
| How to find it | where it appears in the year, month, day or hour of the chart, the noble is there |
| What it does | with it, a life level and steady, few illnesses and few troubles |

### 1.3 The noble of the moon’s virtue

The noble of the moon’s virtue and the noble of heaven’s virtue are called the two virtues, and the old books take them as the sign of a kind and good nature. Where a fierce star, a seven killings or a rob killer, stands in the same pillar as the two virtues, its violence is much reduced — an important line of thought in using the stars in combination.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | born in a yin, wu or xu month, bing; in a shen, zi or chen month, ren; in a hai, mao or wei month, jia; in a si, you or chou month, geng |
| What it does | with the two virtues a person is kindly and generous, wholly sincere with others. A killing carrying the two virtues is clear-minded and decisive and yet generous; eating and wounding carrying them is clever and fine and yet generous. Of the two, the virtue of heaven weighs more and the virtue of the moon less. |

### 1.4 The noble of the pole

It governs a quick and studious mind, fond of the deep subjects. A person with the noble of the pole feels at home in philosophy, history, religion and the old learning.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The verse | jia and yi see the rat and the horse; bing and ding the fowl and the hare and all goes well; wu and ji the four last months of the seasons; geng and xin the tiger and the pig and the salary is rich; ren and gui the snake and the monkey and beauty is preferred. Meet it and fortune gathers. |
| What it does | a quick and studious mind with a gift for digging; fond of letters, history, philosophy and religion. Straight in conduct, and what is begun is finished. |

### 1.5 The star of letters

It governs literary gift and study; a person with it is usually refined in manner and eager to learn. Those who do well in examinations often carry it in the chart.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The verse | jia and yi, si and wu, and the news is good; bing and wu at shen, ding and ji at the fowl; geng the pig, xin the rat, ren meets the tiger; gui meeting the hare climbs the ladder to the clouds. |
| How to find it | from the year stem or the day stem, and where the branch appears in the chart |
| What it does | with it in the chart, clever beyond the common, and ill turned to good; refined in temper, gentle in bearing, hungry for new knowledge, with ambition |

### 1.6 The heavenly doctor

It governs health and the healing art. A person with it, in a strong state, is not only healthy himself but well suited to medicine, psychology, the care of the body and other work that turns on health.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| How to find it | born in the first month, chou; second, yin; third, mao; fourth, chen; fifth, si; sixth, wu; seventh, wei; eighth, shen; ninth, you; tenth, xu; eleventh, hai; twelfth, zi. Found from the month branch, applied to the other branches |
| What it does | in a strong state with a noble generating and helping it, a robust body, and a marked fitness for medical work, for psychology and for philosophy |

## 2. The neutral stars

The neutral stars carry no fixed good or ill of their own; they are judged with the chart in hand.

### 2.1 The post horse

It governs movement, travel and removal. A person with it is restless by nature and cannot sit still. Traders, soldiers, diplomats and salesmen, whose work means travelling or being posted about, often carry it.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a shen, zi or chen chart has its horse at yin; a yin, wu or xu chart at shen; a si, you or chou chart at hai; a hai, mao or wei chart at si |
| How to find it | from the day branch, and where it stands in the chart, there is the horse |
| What it does | the horse is the image of movement and galloping. The person is restless and will travel east, west, south and north. Traders, soldiers and diplomats, who are always on the road, mostly carry it. |

### 2.2 The canopy

A solitary star, but one with depth. A person with it dislikes bustle, prefers to be alone with a thought, and takes naturally to philosophy, religion, the old learning and the arts. Many scholars, cultivators and artists carry it.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a yin, wu or xu chart sees xu; a hai, mao or wei chart sees wei; a shen, zi or chen chart sees chen; a si, you or chou chart sees chou |
| How to find it | from the year branch or the day branch |
| What it does | a quiet temper, fastidious and high-minded, quick in mind, rich in letters and art, drawn to philosophy and religion |

### 2.3 The general star

A star of power, governing the capacity to lead and organise. A person with it has weight enough to hold a crowd and is suited to a post of management. But it must not be dead or cut off, or clashed through, for then the name is there and the substance is not.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a yin, wu or xu chart sees wu; a si, you or chou chart sees you; a shen, zi or chen chart sees zi; a hai, mao or wei chart sees mao |
| How to find it | from the year branch or the day branch, and where it appears in the chart |
| What it does | a star of power, with the gift of leading, organising and commanding, and weight enough to hold a crowd. Dead, cut off or clashed through, it is unfavourable. |

### 2.4 The peach blossom, also called Xianchi

It governs the regard of others and the luck in love, the star best known and most often misread.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a shen, zi or chen chart has it at you; a yin, wu or xu chart at mao; a si, you or chou chart at wu; a hai, mao or wei chart at zi |
| How to find it | from the year branch or the day branch, applied to the other branches |
| A caution | peach blossom with a yang blade: much learned and capable, and yet much illness. A clash on the peach blossom in the year or the luck, and a man offends the law over a woman |

### 2.5 The lu star

Lu is the lin-guan position of the ten stems, standing for rank and fortune. Lu in the year branch is the lu of the year, in the month the founding lu, in the day the private lu, in the hour the returning lu — and the returning lu, in the hour, is the most auspicious, the image of the road to the blue clouds. But lu must not fall into the void or meet a clash, which is called a broken lu, and then clothes and food fall short.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | jia at yin, yi at mao, bing and wu at si, ding and ji at wu, geng at shen, xin at you, ren at hai, gui at zi |
| The four kinds | year branch, the lu of the year; month branch, the founding lu; day branch, the private lu; hour branch, the returning lu |
| A caution | lu in the void or clashed is a broken lu: a noble is suspended from office and stripped of rank, and an ordinary man goes short of clothes and food |

### 2.6 Kuigang

Kuigang is four particular day pillars: ren-chen, geng-xu, geng-chen and wu-xu. A person with it is fierce in temper, straight and decisive, and hates what is wrong with a passion. But it needs a strong day stem to do its work; with a weak day stem kuigang runs into setbacks instead.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The pillars | ren-chen, geng-xu, geng-chen, wu-xu |
| The condition | found in the day pillar, and the day stem must be strong |
| The temper | fierce, straight, bold, plain-spoken, without guile, hating wrong, clever and decisive, good at using power |

### 2.7 The yang blade

The blade is the peak position of a yang stem: jia at mao, bing and wu at wu, geng at you, ren at zi. It is where lu, gone one step too far, gives birth to the blade. The blade is fierce and violent: met by a strong self it is oil on the fire, met by a weak self it is a good thing that holds the self up. With the two virtues in the same pillar its violence can be dissolved.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| How to find it | jia at mao, yi at yin, bing and wu at wu, ding and ji at si, geng at you, xin at shen, ren at zi, gui at hai |
| Its nature | fierce, violent, rousing, impatient. Lu gone too far and the blade is born: the work done, and no withdrawing when it is time |
| A strong self | the blade is not welcome; with the two virtues helping, trouble can be dissolved |
| A weak self | the blade can hold the self up |

## 3. The fierce stars

The fierce stars stand for obstruction, loss and what works against a person. They are for reference only, and should not be read too much into.

### 3.1 The void

The odd one among the fierce stars: it is not bad, it is empty. Seek wealth and meet the void, and it is not to be had; seek office and meet it, and the name is there without the substance. Where the void falls, that pillar’s field of kin is what runs empty.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| How to find it | from the day pillar; where the year, month or hour branch shows it, there is the void |
| What it means | what is sought and not obtained is the void |

The void falling in different pillars empties different sides of a life. The table gives the quick reading:

| Position | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Void in the year branch | thin ancestral regard; an early death among the grandparents, or the family property gone; the mother void, through death, remarriage, running off, or no care for the child |
| Void in the month branch | little sense of family; no brothers or sisters, or brothers and sisters with nothing to lean on |
| Void in the hour branch | unfavourable to children: none, or several lost, or an early death, or poor health |

### 3.2 The rob killer

It stands for force from outside robbing what is yours: taken from without is called a rob. Where it falls, the person or the business of that pillar is easily encroached on by outsiders.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a shen, zi or chen chart sees si; a hai, mao or wei chart sees shen; a yin, wu or xu chart sees hai; a si, you or chou chart sees yin |
| What it means | rob means to take; taken from without is the rob. It stands at the cut-off position among the twelve stages. |

### 3.3 The loss spirit

The opposite of the rob killer: lost from within is called a loss. The rob is outsiders taking; the loss spirit is a fault inside, a wrong decision, a chance thrown away by one’s own hand. But where it stands with a fortunate star, ill may turn to good.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | a yin, wu or xu chart sees si; a hai, mao or wei chart sees yin; a si, you or chou chart sees shen; a shen, zi or chen chart sees hai |
| What it means | loss means to lose; lost from within is the loss. The rob stands where the phase is cut off, the loss where the phase is in force. |
| A caution | the loss spirit in a chart brings no light trouble, and a mind that schemes is never at rest. But standing with a fortunate star, it turns to good instead. |

### 3.4 Heaven’s net and earth’s mesh

Chen is heaven’s net, xu is earth’s mesh; a man fears the net, a woman the mesh. Met in the luck or the year, lawsuits, prison, or grave illness and calamity are to be looked for, and care taken.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The definition | chen is heaven’s net, xu is earth’s mesh; the man fears the net, the woman the mesh |
| What it does | illness and calamity, and prison; met in the luck or the year, it works against a person |

### 3.5 The yin-yang mischance

Twelve particular day pillars, governing a family life out of tune: a woman with it is apt to be at odds with her husband’s family, a man with it to have disputes with his wife’s.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| How to find it | bing-zi, ding-chou, wu-yin, xin-mao, ren-chen, gui-si, bing-wu, ding-wei, wu-shen, xin-you, ren-xu, gui-hai: twelve days |
| In a woman’s chart | little concord with the parents-in-law, want of goodwill among the sisters-in-law, and the husband’s house cools |
| In a man’s chart | a drawing away from the wife’s family, disputes and little concord with them |

### 3.6 The lone phoenix

It governs solitude and a marriage that will not go well. It is found where the day and the hour pillars both carry one of the lone-phoenix days, which is a more careful test than a single star: both positions at once are needed to make the pattern.

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| How to find it | yi-si, ding-si, xin-hai, wu-shen, ren-yin, wu-wu, ren-zi, bing-wu. Any two of these in the day and hour pillars at once, and it is there |
| What it means | solitude, things going badly, and a checking of husband or wife |

## 4. The principle for using the stars

### 4.1 What the *Mingli Yueyan* says

> The good and ill of any fate come from the pattern and the luck; how can one believe in a star because it happens to fall there? — Chen Su’an, *Mingli Yueyan*
> What it means: the good and ill of a life are settled by the pattern and the luck; a coincidental star is no ground for belief.

### 4.2 The right attitude to the stars

- Know which is master and which servant: the generating and checking of the five phases, the relations of the ten gods and the judging of the pattern are the body of the art.
- The stars are the servant: they are a contributing factor, for reference only. They must not take the place of the master, and must not be read too much into.

### 4.3 How to go about learning

- The five phases generating and checking: the core.
- The relations of the ten gods: the foundation.
- Judging the pattern: the crux.
- The stars: a contributing factor, for reference only.

> Chujia Kuang, in sum: the stars are for reference, and should not be read too much into.

## Summary of the lesson

1. The fortunate stars — the noble of heaven’s yi, which turns ill to good; the two virtues, which give generosity; the noble of the pole, a studious mind; the star of letters, cleverness; the heavenly doctor, health.
2. The neutral stars — the post horse, movement; the canopy, philosophy and religion; the general star, power; the peach blossom, the affections; the lu star, rank and fortune; kuigang, fierceness; the yang blade, violence.
3. The fierce stars — the void, what is sought and not had; the rob killer, taken from without; the loss spirit, lost from within; the net and the mesh, prison; the yin-yang mischance, a marriage that will not go; the lone phoenix, solitude.
4. The core principle — the five phases and the ten gods first, the stars as servants; the servant must not take the master’s place.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: the noble of heaven’s yi

For a person with jia wood as the day stem, which branches carry the noble of heaven’s yi?

- A. the rat and the monkey (zi, shen)
- B. the ox and the sheep (chou, wei)
- C. the pig and the fowl (hai, you)
- D. the tiger and the horse (yin, wu)

???+ "Answer"
    B. The verse runs jia and wu take the ox and the sheep: with jia as the day stem, chou and wei in the branches are the noble.

### Exercise 2: the star of letters

What are the usual marks of a person with the star of letters in the chart?

- A. restless, travelling east, west, south and north
- B. clever beyond the common, ill turned to good, refined in bearing
- C. fierce and violent, impatient and impulsive
- D. a marriage that will not go, at odds with the parents-in-law

???+ "Answer"
    B. With the star of letters: clever beyond the common, ill turned to good, refined in bearing, gentle in manner, hungry for new knowledge, with ambition.

### Exercise 3: the void

What does the void in the year branch stand for?

- A. unfavourable to children
- B. thin ancestral regard, the family property gone
- C. much travelling
- D. great power

???+ "Answer"
    B. **Void in the year branch**: thin ancestral regard, an early death among the grandparents or the family property gone. The hour branch touches children, the month branch the sense of family.

### Exercise 4: the principle

According to the *Mingli Yueyan*, where should the emphasis in learning the eight characters fall?

- A. learning the verses of the stars by heart
- B. studying the variations of the net and the mesh
- C. the five phases, the ten gods and the judging of the pattern
- D. memorising how every star is found

???+ "Answer"
    C. **The core principle**: the generating and checking of the five phases, the relations of the ten gods and the judging of the pattern are the body of the art. The stars are for reference, and must not take the master’s place.

### Exercise 5: naming a star

Which star stands for movement, removal and travel?

- A. the canopy
- B. the post horse
- C. kuigang
- D. the peach blossom

???+ "Answer"
    B. **The post horse**: the image of movement and galloping. The canopy leans to philosophy and religion, kuigang to a fierce straight temper, the peach blossom to the affections.
