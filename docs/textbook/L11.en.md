# Lesson 11 · Combinations (II): the Four Fortunate Matched

[简体中文](/textbook/L11/) ｜ [繁體中文](/zh-hant/textbook/L11/) ｜ **English** ｜ [日本語](/ja/textbook/L11/) ｜ [한국어](/ko/textbook/L11/)

## 1. The eating god, and what suits it

> From the *Xiji Pian*: wu earth born in the geng-shen hour is called the place where the eating god is strong in the stem; if the year or month offends with jia, bing, mao or yin, then it is met and not met. An eating god with the strength of one qi gives a deep fortune; meeting an indirect seal that takes it, fortune and years are both reduced.

### 1.1 Its faces

The eating god is the star of longevity and of fortune, what I generate, the natural enemy of the seven killings. Whether a person is healthy and fortunate is read first from it. Among persons it stands for the son in a woman’s chart and for the grandparents; in the mind, for technique, talent, speech, inspiration and the ability to earn; in temper, for consideration, cheerfulness, plainness; in health, for the constitution itself, and an eating god checked means the body will show it. Why is it fortunate? For three reasons: it controls the killing, being their natural enemy, and so guards the body and the years; it generates wealth, turning talent into money; and it drains the finest of the self, showing talent mildly and smoothly. He Zhizhang came home in honour at eighty-six, a steady life of poetry and wine: an eating god with qi, and fortune and years together.

### 1.2 What suits it

**Generating wealth.** Talent turned into money, capacity put to work: the eating god is technique, speech, consideration, and the wealth it generates is income. Generating wealth is talent cashed.

> A case: yi wood as the eating god generating ding fire as wealth. The man works in sales; a strong eating god looks after people and puts them at ease, which is the core of the trade.

**Using the killing.** The seven killings controlled are the indirect officer, and the eating god takes the initiative to control them, turning the killing into authority. A man whose eating god controls the killing can dissolve pressure by his own capacity and turn danger into opening.

> A case: the eating god controlling the killing turns the killing into authority. Talent holds the bad side of the seven killings, the killings are used by the man, and an uncommon career comes of it.

### 1.3 What does not

**The owl taking the food.** The indirect seal is the seven killings of the eating god, and meeting it takes it, which is why the indirect seal is also called inverted food and the owl. It points to prison and lawsuits, sudden illness and loss of money. In a child it means a weak constitution, slow speech and a withdrawn manner. An eating god taken and the body suffers.

> A case: the man has a poor spleen and stomach and frequent headaches; he danced as a child and injured his neck; both parents had their gall bladders removed.

**Using the officer, which does not work.** The eating god meeting the direct officer is not as violent as the wounding officer meeting him, and it damages his dignity. There is no reasoning by which the eating god uses the direct officer, and what it shows is unfitness for public office.

### 1.4 The quick list

- generating wealth, good: talent into money, technique creating value.
- controlling the killing, good: the killing turned into authority, power and decision.
- the owl taking the food, bad: illness, lawsuits, loss of money.
- using the officer, which does not work: it damages the officer’s dignity.

> A note by Chujia Kuang. The eating god is the only one of the four fortunate directly tied to the quality of a life: taken, the body will show it, and there is no hedging. Reading health, look at the eating god first.

## 2. Wealth, and what suits it

> From the *Siyan Dubu*: lu established in the month, one does not stay in the family house; see wealth and officer once and fortune comes of itself. From the *Jishan Pian*: to be rich and honoured comes of strong wealth generating the officer; much wealth with a weak self is the poor man in the rich house. What suits wealth turns entirely on the strength of the self: to a strong self it is welcome, and the fear is the peers dividing it; to a weak self it is a burden, and the peers are welcome as help. In a lu-established pattern the month is lu, strong enough, and wealth or officer must still show above before fortune comes.

### 2.1 Its faces

Wealth feeds a life: what I check, what I own and control. Among persons, the father as indirect wealth, the wife as direct, the mistress as indirect; in money, income, investment, salary, gold and jewels, fixed property, windfalls; in the mind, enjoyment, the fine and the showy, artistic gift; in work, pay and its flexibility. What suits it turns on the strength of the self: strong, it is a fortune; weak, a harm, which is the poor man in the rich house. Fan Li, the lord of Tao, gathered and scattered a fortune three times, rich as a state and coming out whole: wealth with a source and a self strong enough to carry it.

### 2.2 What suits it

**Wealth generating a strong officer to guard the wealth.** Wealth feeds the officer and the officer guards the wealth, with no fear of the peers taking it: wealth and officer both fine. In a woman’s chart strong wealth generating the officer is the sign of a husband prospering.

> A case: wealth generating the officer to guard it, help from the wife’s family, and the money begins to gather after the marriage.

### 2.3 What does not

**Wealth banding with the killing.** Wealth feeding the killing to attack the body brings lawsuits and prison. Trouble easily starts over women or drink; a woman’s marriage goes badly and she meets poor partners. The sign is that money brings trouble rather than ease.

> A case: xin metal as wealth generating gui water as the seven killings: wealth bands with the killing, the killing strong attacks the body, the marriage goes badly and petty men and disputes never stop.

**The peers taking wealth.** Heavy spending, little to keep, money disputes easily. In a man’s chart a third party comes in easily. Either friends borrow and do not repay, or a partnership is cheated, and money comes and goes in a hurry.

> A case: gui water as the peers taking bing fire as wealth; the man has heavy outgoings and keeps little.

### 2.4 The quick list

- generating the officer, good: wealth growing, work going well, a woman prospering her husband.
- banding with the killing, bad: disputes over money or women, lawsuits.
- the peers taking it, bad: loss, money disputes, unstable marriage in a man’s chart.

## 3. The direct officer, and what suits it

> From the *Jishan Pian*: the straight officer star fears punishment and clash; an officer with a seal and no damage is timber for the hall of state. With wealth and seal to support it the officer is noble; damaged by the wounding officer and mixed with the seven killings it is base.

### 3.1 Its faces

The direct officer heads the ten and is the root of honour. In temper it is traditional and steady, valuing rule, and in excess vain. In work it is the state, law and administration. In kin it is the husband in a woman’s chart and children in a man’s. Among persons, superiors, elders and patrons. In health, chronic pressure: strong and uncontrolled, long high strain. The full ten dimensions are in lesson 9; this section is on the combinations. Zhang Liang sat in the tent and set the plan that gave Liu Bang the empire, and then withdrew and went off with the master Chisongzi: the officer of use, advancing and withdrawing in measure.

### 3.2 What suits it

**Wealth coming to generate it.** Wealth is the original spirit of the officer: wealth feeds the officer, the officer guards the wealth, and wealth and officer are both fine, the pattern clean.

> A case: ding fire as wealth generating ji earth as the direct officer, the officer with a source. But yi wood as the wounding officer in the chart checks the direct officer, and so the man is unsuited to public office and his marriage suffers. The case shows both the welcome and the unwelcome at once.

**The seal coming to shield it.** The officer generates the seal and the seal holds off the wounding officer to shield the officer: officer and seal together, a pattern of high order.

> A case: bing fire as the direct officer fed by ji earth as the seal, officer and seal both strong, and the man is a civil servant.
> A second: a gui water day stem born in the xu month, wu earth as the direct officer showing in the month, wealth, officer and seal all strong together and no seven killings mixed in: the highest arrangement of the direct officer, with three chains of energy running at once.

### 3.3 What does not

**The wounding officer damaging it.** The wounding officer checks the direct officer and leaves him powerless: meeting the officer, a hundred troubles, lawsuits and prison.

> A case: in the jia-wu cycle from 2012, with the wu-xu year of 2018, the wounding officer grew greatly and the direct officer was damaged, and the man went to prison. Meeting the officer with no wealth to bridge and no seal to control the wounding, the disaster comes hard and direct.

**The seven killings mixing with it.** The two seen together are like honest and crooked men in one court: work goes badly, petty men and disputes, unstable feeling. Where officer and killing are mixed, the first business is to take out the muddy and keep the clear.

> A case: bing fire as the seven killings and ding fire as the direct officer both showing: officer and killing mixed, the career hard to advance, petty men everywhere.

### 3.4 The quick list

Four questions about the direct officer: is there wealth to feed it, a seal to shield it, a wounding officer to damage it, a seven killings to mix with it? With the four answered, honour and want are plain.

- wealth generating it, good: wealth and officer both fine, the pattern clean.
- the seal shielding it, good: officer and seal together, a high order.
- the wounding officer damaging it, bad: lawsuits and prison.
- the seven killings mixing it, bad: work goes badly, petty men and disputes.

## 4. The seal, and what suits it

> From the *Jishan Pian*: the living seal favours office and fears the country of wealth; a seal injured, and honour does not last. The seal in place means study and name; broken by wealth, greed damages the seal and both study and name are lost.

### 4.1 Its faces

The seal is the source of the qi that generates me, and a strong seal means help and backing. Among persons, the mother, elders, teachers; in things, contracts, papers, diplomas, certificates, cars, houses; in the mind, help from patrons and recognition; in life, the stability of home and work. Its combinations turn on one word, officer: with an officer generating the seal it is noble; with no officer and wealth breaking it, it is straitened. Confucius, teacher of ten thousand generations, three thousand disciples, his virtue reaching down the centuries: the seal in place, learning at its height.

### 4.2 What suits it

**The officer coming to generate it.** The officer is the original spirit of the seal, the officer generates the seal and the seal shields the officer, both strong and the fate noble. What the pair amounts to is being recognised by others, which is not confined to civil servants; selling needs the same energy.

> A case: a strong seal using xin metal as the direct officer hidden in you: the officer generating the seal and the seal shielding the officer, the round running with force. The man graduated from a leading university and rose to a departmental post.

### 4.3 What does not

**Wealth coming to break it.** Wealth breaking the seal is greed damaging it, a small gain for a great loss. At home, money trouble strains the household; at work, pay disputes sap the will; in study, play and romance distract; in marriage, the nearer the wedding the likelier the trouble, often over the elders’ opposition. Wealth and seal are a natural pair of opposites: wealth invents and adapts, the seal conserves and keeps.

> A case: in a chart where wealth breaks the seal, the seal is the root of the household, and a root broken by wealth makes the marriage unstable, ending in divorce over an affair or money.

### 4.4 The quick list

- officer and seal generating one another, good: a noble fate, work accomplished, patrons helping.
- strong seal with a strong self, neutral: step by step, short on flexibility.
- the seal too strong, bad: dependent, no judgment of one’s own.

From Chujia Kuang’s practice, wealth breaking the seal is the great conflict of this lesson: wealth invents and adapts, the seal conserves and keeps, and they are opposed by nature. Parents: at odds, divorced, or one gone early, depending on the rest. Household: money and relations dragging each other down. Study: an early romance and play spoiling the work, results unsteady. Feeling: money and family opposition spoiling the marriage, easily ending in divorce. Work: pay disputes unsettling the job. Partnership: an oversight bringing dispute, loss or a lawsuit. And there are more images to be found.

Two keys to it. First, the officer star as the bridge: wealth generates the officer and the officer generates the seal, so wealth does not break the seal but officer and seal generate one another into a noble pattern, and where the chart has no officer a cycle bringing one is the turn. Second, the peers taking the wealth to shield the seal: let the peers divide the wealth so that it cannot check the seal.

## 5. The lesson in view: one substance, different combinations, different conclusions

### 5.1 The chain of energy

Food generates wealth, wealth generates the officer, the officer generates the seal: the four fortunate make one chain, link on link, and break one and all are broken.

The eating god taken, and the source of wealth is cut and the body suffers. Wealth robbed, and the officer has nothing to feed it and the money will not stay. The direct officer damaged, and the seal loses its guard and lawsuits follow. The seal broken, and the root shakes and household and study are both lost.

### 5.2 The four together

The four sections keep saying one thing: the same god in different combinations is a different fate.

- Eating god: generating wealth and controlling the killing is good, the owl taking it is bad. It is fortune, and taken the fortune ends.
- Wealth: generating the officer and guarding wealth is good, banding with the killing and being robbed is bad. Wealth wants the officer’s guard, and unguarded it breaks.
- Direct officer: supported by wealth and seal it is noble, mixed with wounding officer and killing it is base. The officer wants support, and alone it is in danger.
- Seal: generated by the officer it is noble, broken by wealth it is straitened. The seal wants the officer to feed it, and unfed it is straitened.

### 5.3 Three tests

First, the combination: are the neighbours feeding it or taking it? Fortune lies not in the god but in the company it keeps.

Second, the root: shown and rooted means a deep fortune, floating without a root a thin one. A fortunate god with a root is truly fortunate.

Third, the chain: food, wealth, officer, seal. Whatever link is checked, that field of life suffers.

### 5.4 Wealth and seal, and one body many faces

This lesson keeps returning to the opposition of wealth and seal, and that opposition is the liveliest footnote to one body many faces.

The same wealth star with an officer is noble. The same seal with an officer is noble too, and with wealth straitened. The wealth has not changed and neither has the seal; what changed is whom they met. And deeper: wealth meeting the seal is not always bad. Where wealth is the master, wealth wearing the seal takes its nobility from the seal; where the seal is the master, greed breaking it shows at once. The difference is the pattern, and what a pattern is will be taken up in the advanced course.

That is the heart of it: no god is good or bad in itself, and fortune lies in what stands beside it. The way of the four fortunate is not in the gods but in whether the combinations can be seen through.

> The way of the four fortunate lies not in the gods but in their company. One substance, different combinations, different conclusions: the deepest working of one body many faces in the patterns of the ten gods.
> A note by Chujia Kuang. Having finished the four, take your own chart and go over it: is the eating god taken, the wealth robbed, the direct officer damaged, the seal broken? Whatever link is wrong, that field of life wants watching. Reading a chart is not reciting formulae; it is putting yourself inside the chain and finding the break.

## Summary of the lesson

1. Eating god: the star of longevity and fortune, the natural enemy of the seven killings; it likes generating wealth and using the killing, and dislikes the owl taking it and does not use the officer.
2. Wealth: the source that feeds a life, to be carried by a strong self; it likes food and wounding to generate it and the officer to guard it, and dislikes the peers taking it and banding with the killing.
3. Direct officer: the root of honour and head of the ten; it likes wealth to feed it and the seal to shield it, and dislikes the wounding officer damaging it and the killing mixing with it.
4. Seal: the mother that generates the self and the root of the qi; it likes the officer to feed it, and dislikes wealth breaking it.
5. One body many faces: the same god in different combinations gives different conclusions. Food with wealth is riches, with the owl it is taken; wealth with the officer is riches, with the rob it breaks; the officer with wealth and seal is noble, with wounding officer and killing base; the seal with the officer is noble, with wealth broken.

## Practice

### Exercise 1: the four

Which group is made up entirely of the four fortunate?

1. direct officer, seven killings, eating god, wealth
2. eating god, wealth, direct officer, seal
3. seven killings, wounding officer, owl, yang blade
4. wounding officer, eating god, wealth, direct officer

???+ "Answer"
    B. The four fortunate are the eating god, wealth, the direct officer and the seal.

### Exercise 2: the eating god

What does the eating god most like, and what does it most fear?

???+ "Answer"
    It most likes generating wealth, which cashes talent, and using the killing, which holds the seven killings with talent and turns them into authority. It most fears the owl taking the food, the indirect seal checking it, which takes the fortune and shortens the years.

### Exercise 3: the direct officer

What are the four questions asked of the direct officer?

???+ "Answer"
    Is there wealth to feed it, a seal to shield it, a wounding officer to damage it, a seven killings to mix with it? Feeding, shielding, the hidden arrow and the inner disorder.
