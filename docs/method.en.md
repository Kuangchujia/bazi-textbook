# One Body, Many Faces

One body, many faces means that the same qi of the great ultimate divides into yin and yang according to movement and stillness; placed in a concrete chart, its weight and direction change with the dimension from which it is observed.

## The three steps

**Fix the dimension** If the dimension is not fixed, the judgements are bound to be confused. Ask first which face you are looking at: character, the six relations, health or career. Take the same direct officer: for character, look at the sense of justice; for marriage, look at whether the husband star is in its proper place; for health, look at whether the officer star is clashed and overcome. This step corresponds to lessons 3—9, the heavenly stems, earthly branches and the images of the ten gods.

**Weigh the force** If the force is wrong, the whole chart is ruined. The same ten god, strong or weak, makes a world of difference. A direct officer with force is authority; without force, an empty title. Wealth with a strong body means riches; with a weak body, a burden. This step corresponds to lessons 14—15, strength and decline, restraint and support.

**Observe the combination** Different combinations make entirely different chart patterns. The ten gods never act alone. The direct officer meeting the resource gives resource and officer generating each other; meeting the wounding officer gives the wounding officer confronting the officer. This step corresponds to lessons 10—12, the three-part sequence on combinations of the ten gods.

## What is wrong with judging from one point

See friend or rob wealth and think only of wealth being seized; see seven killings and think only of disaster. Yet friend and rob wealth can seize wealth and can also be partners; seven killings attacks the body, but when controlled it becomes the indirect officer.

Lesson 13 has an example: in one chart the resource is damaged by wealth. For schooling, the resource damaged by wealth is unlucky; for career, one must see whether the officer star has force. The same chart, change the field and the judgement changes. If you keep judging only from "wealth damages the resource", the career side is erased.

Good is not always good, bad is not wholly bad; it all lies between the angle and the combination.

## Scope

The three steps are a way of reading a chart, not a formula for judgements. Reaching a conclusion before the dimension is fixed, or applying one ten god to every field, is outside the use of one body, many faces. It gives readings of the same chart at different levels; it gives no ready-made judgements.
