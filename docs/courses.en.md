# Courses and Teaching Services

The textbook is public; teaching is a separate matter. This page states only the current situation and its boundaries; it lists no prices and provides no purchase or enrolment entry.

## Three levels

| Level | Textbook | Scope | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation course | Four parts, seventeen lessons, 120,000+ characters | Charting, yin and yang and the five phases, the heavenly stems and earthly branches, the ten gods, chart patterns, strength and decline, ten-year luck and yearly luck | Textbook published, free to read |
| Advanced seminar | Eighteen chapters, more than 240,000 characters | Taking the pattern, the useful god, the ministering god, body and function, success, failure and rescue, punishments, clashes and combinations, the luck of the years, and the special chart patterns | Separate book; fee-paying enrolment not started for now |
| Disciple class | The third level | According to the formal teaching materials | Not open for now |

The seventeen lessons of the foundation course are the whole of the [textbook text](textbook/L01.md) on this site; the eighteen chapters of the advanced seminar are not on this site and are arranged separately.

## Fees and enrolment

The exact form, opening times and fees of the foundation course and the advanced seminar have not been settled; fee-paying enrolment is not started for now; the disciple class is not open for now. ## Learning path

Read the textbook first and learn to set out a chart correctly; then work through the exercises lesson by lesson to make the concepts clear. The order of the foundation course is charting, yin and yang and the five phases, the heavenly stems and earthly branches, the ten gods, chart patterns, strength and decline, ten-year luck and yearly luck; the advanced seminar presupposes the foundation course, and the sequence of judgements on taking the pattern and the useful god rests on familiarity with stems and branches, the ten gods and strength and decline.

The textbook is arranged in the original lesson order and can be studied independently. Whether to follow a course depends on how much feedback one needs, not on whether the content is available.
