# Foundation Textbook of the Four Pillars and Eight Characters, Seventeen Lessons · Practice Booklet (No Answers)

## How to use this booklet

This booklet copies the question texts of the "practice" sections on the seventeen public lesson pages of the *Foundation Textbook of the Four Pillars and Eight Characters*, lesson by lesson, without changing the words or composing new questions.
The folded answers originally in the textbook are not included at all; where the original question has no answer, this booklet supplies none.
To check the source, follow the original address under each lesson heading.
This site provides material for academic discussion only, not specific fortune-telling.
