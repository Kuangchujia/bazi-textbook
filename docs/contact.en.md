# Contact and Corrections

The textbook, exercises and appendices can all be studied on your own; no need to hold on to a stuck point. This page has only three things: how to get in touch, what you can ask, and what is out of scope.

## Contact

**Email**: chirca@163.com

Corrections are best sent by email, so that you can attach the original passage, a screenshot and the proposed change.

**WeChat**: jiayanyide

The nickname is "Jiayanyide", in Jieyang, Guangdong. Scanning the code and searching for the WeChat ID reach the same account.

![WeChat QR code: Jiayanyide](assets/wechat-contact.png)

When adding, please state your purpose, for example "textbook correction" or "lesson 14, strength and decline", so that we can tell you apart.

## What you can ask

- Questions about the textbook: a judgement you cannot read, a deduction that does not connect, an example that does not match the main text.
- Errors and wording problems: quotations, years, table values, example stems and branches. Such changes are entered in the version record in [Rights and version notes](rights.md).
- Enquiries about courses already offered and teaching services; for the current state see [Courses and teaching services](courses.md).

When reporting a correction, please give the lesson number, the section heading and the exact paragraph. The numbering of the seventeen lessons and their sections is stable; the entry has to match before it can be checked. A screenshot or the original sentence is even better, and saves one round trip.

## Out of scope

This is a personal, static site. There are no entry points for purchases, orders, payments or enrolment, and no appointment placeholders.

The textbook and teaching provide only theoretical training and discussion of methods; they do not involve specific fortune-telling services. Requests for individual fate readings, charting and date selection are therefore not accepted here; messages will not be answered. Nor will we reply to commercial promotion, mutual promotion or delegated operation unrelated to the textbook.

Individual analysis and course enquiries are handled at [the Shushu site contact page](https://jiayanyide.com/contact/).
